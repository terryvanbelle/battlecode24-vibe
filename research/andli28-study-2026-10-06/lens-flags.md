# Lens flags

## The map decides most of the matchup. Win rates differ between maps far beyond chance, and 15 maps produced no win at all. (strong)

Evidence: 1,520 filler games, 449 won (29.5%), 78 maps of 10-29 games each. Chi2 for map heterogeneity is 462 on 77 df. The true between-map SD of win rate is about 0.24 (method of moments: observed variance 0.068 against 0.011 expected from sampling). 15 maps went 0-for (265 games, 17% of games, 25% of losses): Alien, Alligator, BigDucksBigPond, Canals, DefaultLarge, Digging, EvilGrin, Funnel, Gated, Hockey, Racetrack, Soccer, Tunnels, Valentine, Whirlpool. The best maps: Battlefield 10/10, Pancakes 16/17, Bunkers 17/19, Capacitance 21/27, DefaultHuge 19/25, Fusbol 14/19. Map features came from engine/maps/*.map24 (scratch Java against the engine's map loader).

Confound: Per-map n is small (SE about 0.10 a map), so single-map rates are noisy; the heterogeneity test and the feature fits pool across maps. Random side assignment gives uneven side splits within a map.

## Side does not matter, overall or within maps. The extreme single-map side splits are chance. (strong)

Evidence: Side A 214/760 = 0.282 +- 0.016, side B 235/760 = 0.309 +- 0.017. Within-map stratified difference B minus A is +0.024. A permutation test of within-map side heterogeneity (2,000 shuffles of side within map) gives p 0.15. 2 of 78 maps reach Fisher p < 0.05 (QueenOfHearts A 2/11 vs B 7/9; Intercontinental A 8/16 vs B 0/8), at or below the about 4 expected by chance. B is ahead by 2-3 pp within each symmetry class (rotational, vertical, horizontal), never significantly. Weakest hint: level-sum losses are 29.1% of games as A vs 24.9% as B (z about 1.8).

Confound: None material: the filler assigns maps and sides at random.

## The spread of our own spawn zones is the strongest single map predictor, and it acts through our offence. On symmetric maps it equals the spread of andli's spawns, and andli never moves its flags, so it is the spread of andli's flags. On compact maps its three flags sit inside one respawn fortress and our grabs collapse; its captures do not change. (strong)

Evidence: Map-level binomial logit with a map-cluster bootstrap: +0.48 logit per SD of spread (SD 10.3 tiles), z 4.1. With area, path, crumbs, water, wall and symmetry in the fit: +0.45, z 2.0 (area falls to +0.19, z 0.9). Win rate by max own-spawn spread: under 18 tiles 0.139 (230 games, 12 maps), 18-25 0.238 (425), 25-36 0.297 (380), 36 or more 0.419 (485). Across these bins (all games): our captures 0.57 / 0.79 / 1.23 / 1.56, andli's 1.39 / 1.26 / 1.39 / 1.51; our first grabs 4.1 -> 13.1. Conversion per grab is flat (about 0.09). Andli robots near its flags at r300 (survey defend300): 12.6 under 18 tiles vs 6.4 at 36 or more (ours 5.5 / 5.8). Andli defenders within dist2 20 at our grabs: 6.2 vs 3.3. Andli stuns freeze 2,859 of our robots a game on spread under 25 vs 2,101 at 36 or more (full-length games). The full-length score lines on spread under 25 are mostly 0-0 (148 of 478) and 0-1 (116). The same gradient appears against the other four upper-tier bots (g6/g7 censuses, 480 games): 0.11 / 0.18 / 0.28. Against the 20-bot band it is flat (0.71 / 0.67 / 0.75). So this is our offence failing against strong defenders on clustered flags, not something peculiar to andli.

Confound: Spread correlates with area (r 0.58) and dams (0.38). Defenders near flags at r300 and at grabs are partly outcomes of how the fight goes. The figures for the upper-tier bots pool g_iter6 and g_iter7 censuses.

## Loss type is sorted by map class: level-sum losses come on long maps, capture-all losses on short maps, more-flags losses on compact-spawn maps. (strong)

Evidence: Separate map-level logits for P(loss of each type), map bootstrap, per SD. Level-sum: path +0.44 (z 3.5), vertical +0.49 (z 2.3). Capture-all: path -0.55 (z -2.2), area -0.42 (z -1.8). More-flags: spread -0.67 (z -3.3). Path = spawn-to-spawn BFS length, water passable; SD 11.1. Level-sum share of losses rises from 0.24 at path 12-17 to 0.57 at path 30 or more. On path 30 or more (14 maps, 272 games) 89% of games reach r2000, 59% of those end flag-tied, 0-0 is 42% of full-length games, and andli captures 0.62 a game after r600 (1.27 on path under 17). The level gap has the same shape in every class (metrics file, 135 full-length games): -12 at r200, about -50 by r800, partly recovered to -27 +- 5 (long) to -48 +- 3 (path 17-30) at r2000.

Confound: Loss type is mechanically tied to game length (long maps reach r2000), so totals are compared within full-length games where shown. Path length is not observable to the bot (unseen terrain); the observable proxy, Chebyshev distance between spawn centres, is weaker: at 30 or more, 25 maps, flag-tied 0.38, andli late captures 0.92.

## No map class concentrates level-sum losses enough for a map-gated level lever to pay much. Deficits are large everywhere. (moderate)

Evidence: Of all games, 31% end flag-tied and we win 12% of those. The median level deficit in level-sum losses is 42 (q25 27). For the class the bot can observe (Chebyshev to the nearest enemy spawn 30 or more: 25 maps, 452 games), +15 levels would flip 3.5% of those games (+1.1 pp overall) and +25 would flip 7.1% (+2.1 pp). Only path 30 or more (not observable) reaches +8.1 pp on its 272 games at +15. Andli's setup level (andli level200) and its crumbs at r250 are uncorrelated with map win rate (map-level r -0.06 and 0.09).

Confound: Flips assume the extra levels arrive without costing captures, which the closed g7dig and g7bank arms suggest they do not.

## Vertical-symmetry maps (engine VERTICAL = our FX: teams left and right) are bad for us against andli only. It is not a symmetry-detection bug and not geometry. The mechanism is unidentified. (moderate)

Evidence: Win rate: vertical 0.195 (575 games, 30 maps), rotational 0.366 (604, 32), horizontal 0.340 (341, 16). Map-bootstrap logit for vertical: -0.87 (z -2.9), and -0.92 (z -2.7) with size, spread, path, crumbs, water and wall controlled. The penalty holds within every spread class (rotational minus vertical: 0.17, 0.20, 0.16). Against the band, g_iter7 shows none (vertical 208/287 = 0.72 vs rotational 191/274 = 0.70); against Gymhgy (g_iter5, 560 games) none; against the other four upper-tier bots none (0.16 vs 0.18). Front width vs depth is the same (vertical 0.94, rotational 0.95). The penalty persists on the 23 vertical maps where symmetry is decided early (0.228 vs rotational 0.354). Signature on vertical maps: our share of kills that pay us (andli deaths on its own territory) 0.12 vs 0.26 rotational; our first grabs 5.8 vs 9.2. Andli keeps 0-5 robots in our half at r250 in 40% of vertical games (rotational 21%); in those games we win 0.12 on vertical maps vs 0.52 on rotational.

Confound: Correlation only. With 30 vertical maps, an unmeasured map feature cannot be excluded, and andli's behaviour on those maps has not been traced in replays.

## Symmetry is decided late on 15 maps, but timing is a marker of hard maps, not a cause of losses. The pooled basics bars pass while Soccer fails them on its own; only Gauntlet plausibly costs anything. (moderate)

Evidence: Maps with median symDecidedRound over 50 (15 maps, 305 games) win about 12% vs about 32% elsewhere. Within maps that have both, late-decided games do no worse than early ones (stratified difference -0.025; 91 early vs 173 late games). Basics bars on this census: decided by r201 in 1,190/1,203 setup-decidable games (98.9%, bar 95%); by r400 in 298/317 on POST_SETUP_MAPS (94.0%, bar 90%). Per map: Soccer is undecided at r400 in 14 of 16 games (median r514), Gauntlet in 4 of 17 (median r365). Distance between the wrongly guessed enemy-centre set (best(): rotation first) and the true set: 1 tile on Soccer, Asteroids, Digging, HungerGames, MazeRunner, Snake, Fusbol (harmless); 34 tiles on Gauntlet (4/17 wins; decided by r20 2/2, later 2/15); 17-33 tiles on Tunnels, Gated and Racetrack, but only until about r100-203. symWrong is 0 in all 1,520 games.

Confound: The within-map test has little power, and on Soccer and Gauntlet the timing hardly varies. Gauntlet is 1.1% of games.

## Our flags carried 6 or more tiles from their spawn in setup are captured more often than flags left near the spawn, even at the same distance from andli's spawns. This fits a loss of respawn defence: andli's unmoved flags are guarded by its respawns. (moderate)

Evidence: Premise dump on the VM: --flags on 75 games with 1-2 andli captures (45 compact-spread, 30 wide), 225 of our flags; displacement measured from the flag's origin (flag id = origin tile) to its r200 placement. Within strata of final distance to the nearest andli spawn centre, flags displaced 6 or more tiles are captured +0.161 more often (Mantel-Haenszel; game-clustered bootstrap SE 0.060, z 2.7); same sign on compact and wide maps. Capture rate by displacement: under 4 tiles 0.46 (compact) / 0.30 (wide); 14 or more tiles 0.59 / 0.71, although those flags sit farther from andli (35.8 / 49.3 tiles vs 25.0 / 27.2). In the 42 single-capture games, andli took our most-displaced flag 20 times (14.0 expected, one-sided binomial p 0.038). Current displacement: median 6.3 tiles, q90 14.4; 49% of flags under 6. In one Anchor game the flag taken had been left 2 tiles from its spawn, so the effect is a tendency.

Confound: Games were selected for having 1-2 andli captures, so this is conditional on a capture. Relocation moves the most exposed flag first: captured flags started nearer andli (origin 21.7 vs 26.5 tiles on compact maps), and stratifying on final distance only partly removes that. Displacement may also proxy for edge or corner terrain on andli's raid routes. Observational.

## The map-level correlates of our win rate are fight outcomes, not setup economy. (moderate)

Evidence: Correlation of map win rate with per-map means (78 maps, weighted by games): our share of kills that pay us +0.66; (deaths minus kills) per 1,000 rounds -0.62; our first grabs per 1,000 rounds +0.60; andli's paid-kill share -0.56; our robots frozen by its stuns per 1,000 rounds -0.48; andli flag spread +0.43; andli traps per 1,000 rounds -0.37; andli crumbs at r250 +0.09; andli level200 -0.06. Crumbs gathered are at parity in every map class (r1-200 and r201-400 about equal, e.g. 2,913 vs 2,672 and 2,610 vs 2,410 on 3,000-9,000-crumb centres). The economy gap is spending: we hold 186-253 crumbs at r250, andli 2,292-12,088.

Confound: Correlation only. Most of these variables are consequences of who wins the fight, not causes.

## Levers

- **FLAGS_IN_RESPAWN_REACH (relocate only within respawn reach)**: Andli's unmoved flags sit on its spawn zones, so its respawns defend them: 12.6 robots near its flags at r300 on compact maps vs our 5.5, and 6.2 vs 5.2 defenders at grabs. Our RELOC_V2 plus climb carries flags a median 6.3 tiles (q90 14.4) away from our spawns. Flags displaced 6 or more tiles are captured +0.16 more often at equal distance from andli (z 2.7). Switch-level change in src/bot: C.RELOC_R2 225 -> 36 and C.CLIMB_R2 400 -> 36, so a flag's spot stays within 6 tiles of its spawn centre. RELOC_V2 still picks the spot farthest from the nearest enemy spawn under every live symmetry inside that disc, and CLIMB_SEP2 64 is kept (the engine resets flags under dist2 36). This shrinks the T2 relocation. The closed relocation-dose arms (g2reloc2, g5climb3) went the other way (farther); no andli arm has tested keeping flags near the spawns. Signature: r200 flag displacement from origin: median at most 4 tiles and at least 90% of flags under 6 (now median 6.3, 49% under 6). Census vs andli: defNearAtGrab20 (ours) +1.0 or more; survey defend300 (ours) up; enemyFirstGrabs per game -20%; enemyCaptured 1.38 -> 1.17 or less (-15%). Guard on the band, because relocation was shipped for waffle (g_iter3) and Gymhgy (g_iter6): enemyCaptured on the band not up more than 10%. Expected: If the per-flag premise holds, about 0.15-0.2 fewer andli captures a game, converting some 0-1, 1-2 and capture-all losses. Rough estimate +2 to +4 pp against andli; it could be negative on the band against waffle-type raiders. Confidence moderate (observational premise from 75 dumped games). A 5(a) on compact- and wide-spread cells should show the displacement and defNearAtGrab20 shift first.
- **SYM_SCOUT (decide symmetry by sighting after setup)**: Basics (rule 15). On Soccer and Gauntlet symmetry stays undecided long after setup (median r514 and r365; undecided at r400 in 14/16 and 4/17 games). The pooled bar passes and hides this. On Gauntlet, best()'s rotation-first guess puts the enemy centres 34 tiles from the true ones, which feeds carrier-destination prediction (carrierTarget, G.nearest(last, ec)), nearTheirSpawn and ourHalf. Switch: new C.SYM_SCOUT. After r200, while !Sym.decided(), the one field duck nearest to it targets the image under best() of the spawn centre whose images differ most between the surviving candidates (one sight of a spawn-zone tile decides, Sym rule 3) and returns to normal play once it has seen it. At most one scout at a time. Signature: symDecidedRound at r260 or earlier in at least 95% of games on each POST_SETUP_MAPS map, per map not pooled (now Soccer 2/16 by r400, Gauntlet 13/17); symWrong stays 0; no change elsewhere (the scout never fires on 63 maps). Per-map rows added to the basics 'sym late' bar. Expected: Small. It matters only where the wrong set is far (Gauntlet: 17 of 1,520 games, 4 won; Tunnels, Gated and Racetrack until about r100-200): at most +0.1 to +0.3 pp against andli. Value: it closes a per-map basics hole cheaply, and the mechanism is near-certain.
- **FARM_LONG (LEVEL_FARM gated on spawn separation)**: Level-sum losses concentrate where the flags stay level: path 30 or more ends flag-tied in 59% of full-length games, andli captures 0.62 a game after r600 there vs 1.27 on short maps, and deficits are smaller (median 34, q25 19). The bot cannot see path length, so gate on what it can observe once symmetry is decided: Chebyshev distance from our spawn centres to the nearest enemy centre, 30 or more (25 maps, 30% of games). Switch: C.LEVEL_FARM on with FARM_ROUND 1200 and FARM_XP 10 only when that distance is 30 or more (new C.FARM_MIN_CHEB = 30); unchanged elsewhere. This is a map gate on a parked switch (g4farm2/3), not a new mechanism. It overlaps the closed g7dig/g7bank and is offered only because the gate moves the cost (late captures) onto maps where they are rarer. Signature: On gated maps: digsLate 60 or more a game and levelGain1200 +15 vs g_iter7; levelGapEnd up by 15 or more; enemyCaptured after r1200 within +-0.1. On ungated maps: digsLate stays 0 (the gate is silent). Expected: Weak. With the observable gate, +15 levels flips 3.5% of gated games (+1.1 pp overall) and +25 flips 7.1% (+2.1 pp), and only if the farm costs no captures. Andli's late captures on gated maps (0.92 a game after r600) are only modestly below the rest (1.11). Rank last, or use it to test a stronger level lever.
