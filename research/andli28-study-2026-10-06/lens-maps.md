# Lens maps

## Our capture deficit against andli28 comes from how many chains we start, not from how well we convert them. Per chain, both bots convert at the same rate in every window. After r600 we start about half as many chains as it does, and about a third as many with a big grab group. (strong)

Evidence: I dumped all 1,520 filler replays on the VM (--track --recall-d0 --flags, plus a scratch OffDump with per-carrier-round lines). A chain runs from a first grab to its CAPTURE or RETURN. That gives 12,924 of our chains (8.5 a game) and 16,275 of its chains (10.7 a game). Rates below are per 100 rounds over the games still alive, ours then its. Chains: r201-300 1.59 vs 0.48; r301-600 0.84 vs 0.92; r601-900 0.45 vs 0.76; r901-1200 0.42 vs 0.59; r1201-1500 0.34 vs 0.69; r1501-1800 0.33 vs 0.63; r1801-2000 0.25 vs 0.35. Chains with 8 or more of the carrier team within dist2 20 of the flag at the grab (g0>=8): 0.505 vs 0.116 in r201-300, then 0.04-0.08 vs 0.10-0.16 after r600. Conversion per chain in the same windows: .175/.189, .131/.121, .114/.105, .093/.107, .106/.139, .120/.149, .201/.224 (binomial SE about .005-.01). Captures per 100 rounds: ours 0.278 (r201-300), 0.110, 0.051, then 0.037-0.040; its 0.09-0.11 throughout. By r600 we lead 0.56 to 0.34; after r1200 it leads 0.61 to 0.28.

Confound: Starting a chain needs our robots at its flags, and that follows who wins the fight (other lenses). The windows condition on the game still being alive. The two bots' conversion curves were compared marginally, not matched on the fight state.

## After r600 our presence at its flag homes collapses, while its presence at ours grows. Even when a group of ours is within 8 tiles, we close to within 4.5 tiles less often than it does at our flags, because we are outnumbered there. (strong)

Evidence: Each flag lying at home was sampled every 5 rounds (about 1.1M samples per side). Share of samples with at least one of ours within dist2 20 of its home: 11.4% (r201-300), 9.1%, 5.0%, 4.5% (r1201-2000). Its robots at our homes: 5.0%, 8.4%, 8.5%, 10.2%. With 3 or more of ours within dist2 64, the chance that one is within dist2 20 is 0.38/0.37 after r600, against its 0.47/0.46 at our flags. In those hover samples it has 10.3 robots within dist2 64 to our 6.9-7.2. Only 6% of them give us a lead of 2 or more. Its flags are lightly held most of the time: 64% of samples have at most one of its robots within dist2 20. When we grab with a small group, 25 of its 42 live robots are more than 20 tiles from the flag.

Confound: Presence is mostly a product of the fight front: it fights in our half, with a paid-kill share of 0.54 against our 0.20. A low presence may be a symptom, not a separate cause.

## The size of the grab group sets chain conversion, and the curve is nearly the same for both bots. 43% of our chains start with 3 or fewer of ours near the flag. The grabber's HP matters only for those small groups. (strong)

Evidence: Our chain conversion by g0: 0-1 4.6% (n 2,139); 2-3 6.0% (3,458); 4-5 10.0%; 6-7 15.8%; 8-9 21.8%; 10-11 34.6%; 12-13 40.9%; 14-15 42.7%; 16+ 49.4%. Its curve: 3.7%, 6.5%, 12.1%, 19.4%, 28.0%, 26.6%, 35.5%, 36.1%, 37.0%. By margin (g0 minus its robots within dist2 20 at t0): 0 or less 7.4%; +2 13.6%; +4 20.5%; +6 28.5%; +8 34.4%. For g0 0-3, conversion by grabber HP at the first carried round is 2.4% under 334, 4.6% at 334-667 and 9.4% at 668 or more. For g0 4-7 it is 10.2% / 10.8% / 15.1%. For g0 8+ there is no gradient. 48% of our chains start with the grabber under 500 HP.

Confound: g0 and HP are recorded at the end of the pickup round. HP includes the hits taken in that round, so part of the HP gradient runs the other way. Large groups occur when we are winning locally.

## Our carriers die almost on the spot, mostly to its fresh respawns: its flags never leave its spawn centres. Its carriers die the same way to ours, so this is symmetric and not an andli edge. (strong)

Evidence: 0 of 4,560 of its flag homes moved from the spawn centre. Our failed trips (22,852): mean 7.4 rounds carried, 2.8 moves and 2.2 BFS tiles of progress. 61% die with 1 tile of progress or less. The median failed trip lasts 3 rounds (quartiles 1-7). Each takes 3.6 hits from 2.5 attackers; 37.5% of the hits come from its robots spawned 10 or fewer rounds earlier (20% from 3 or fewer). 1.45 fresh enemies stand within dist2 8 at death. 26% are stunned within dist2 13. Mean HP at pickup is 450, against 842 for capturing trips. Its failed trips (35,233): 6.5 rounds, 2.4 tiles, 46% of hits from our fresh spawns, 25.5% stunned. Captures per pickup: ours 6.9% (1,691 of 24,543), its 5.7% (2,117 of 37,350). Its walk from its first grabs is longer: median 29 BFS tiles against our 24.

Confound: Fresh-spawn hits partly reflect where grabs happen (inside spawn zones), not only its spawn choice.

## The tiebreak turns most late ties into losses, and in tied endgames we start few chains even though late conversion is the best of the game. (strong)

Evidence: At r1800, 539 of 1,520 games (35%) are tied on flags. We win 17.8% of them: 358 LEVEL_SUM losses against 43 wins and 3 MORE_BREAD. After r1800 we capture in 10.4% of these games and it captures in 17.1%. Our chains in r1801-2000 of tied games: 0.23 per 100 rounds (g0>=8: 0.049) at 19.8% conversion; its: 0.31 at 21.3%. Behind by one at r1800: 283 games, won 2.5%. Ahead by one at r1500: 216 games, won 69.4%; it erases the lead in 38.9%, and 45 of the 66 losses are LEVEL_SUM. Both teams buy CAPTURING at r1800 in 1,125 games. Re-grab after a carrier drop, ours vs its: 0.51 vs 0.58 (to r1200); 0.49 vs 0.63 (r1201-1800); 0.58 vs 0.71 (CAPTURING window, where dropped flags stay 25 rounds).

Confound: Games still tied at r1800 are stalemate maps or sides; an all-in could change its play too. The level-sum loss rate comes from g_iter7's current economy.

## andli builds stuns beside its own carriers about 6 times as often as we do. After controlling for context, these escort stuns are not linked to its own conversion, so they are not what drives its captures. (moderate)

Evidence: Post-setup stuns built within dist2 36 of the builder team's own carrier: its 18.0 a game (0.87 per trip; 30.5% of trips have at least one), ours 2.1 (0.15 per trip; 8.4%). Escort stun victims: its 110 a game, ours 12.7. Overall it builds 540 post-setup stuns, 534 of them with one of ours within dist2 20, and 74% outside its own half. We build 219, 58% of them within dist2 64 of our flag homes (rings). Trips of 6 or more rounds, stratified by chasers, escorts, HP, window and first grab (Mantel-Haenszel risk difference) with vs without an escort stun in the first 5 rounds: ours +3.5 +- 1.5 pp (917 of 8,413 trips); its -0.2 +- 0.7 pp (5,176 of 11,255).

Confound: Escort stuns need escorts with crumbs and a ready action nearby. Our +3.5 pp may be residual escort strength.

## Levers

- **C.LATE_ALLIN: a score-aware endgame assault when tied or behind at r1800**: We lose 89% of level-sum tiebreaks, so in games tied at r1800 (35% of games) defending buys almost nothing. A capture by it only turns a likely level loss into a flag loss, while a capture by us wins. From r1800 CAPTURING makes late chains the best of the game: 20% conversion, 38% at g0>=8, and a 25-round re-grab window. Yet we start only 0.23 chains per 100 rounds and re-grab 58% of drops (its 71%). The switch, with ALLIN_ROUND=1800, fires when round >= ALLIN_ROUND and our captures (EF_STATE) are no more than its captures (FLAG_LOST lostMask count). Then non-defenders skip alerts and all target one live enemy flag through a shared slot (the nearest to our spawn centroid). A dropped enemy flag within dist2 100 becomes the movement goal (REGRAB-style, only inside this window). Within dist2 64 of that flag, ADVANCE_MARGIN drops to 1. Defenders and the chase of an enemy carrier within CHASE_RADIUS2 stay as now. Not the closed lines: g7bank was about levels, CAPTURING-first moves the upgrade, and forward drift and never-retreat apply all game and ignore the score. Signature: Fire: note 'allin' on turns after r1800, only in games tied or behind. Identity check: games not tied at r1800 are identical up to r1800. Delivery vs andli, from replays of games tied at r1800: our chains per 100 rounds in r1801-2000 from 0.23 to at least 0.45; g0>=8 chains from 0.049 to at least 0.10; re-grab after a drop in the CAPTURING window from 0.58 to at least 0.68; P(we capture after r1800 | tied) from 0.104 to at least 0.18. Outcome: win rate among games tied at r1800 from 0.178 to at least 0.24; its capture rate after r1800 may rise by up to 0.10. Expected: About +0.08 to +0.10 captures per tied game, which is +5 to +7 pp wins among the 35% of games tied at r1800, or about +2 pp overall against andli (at most ~3 pp). The downside is limited to the 46 of 539 tied games we now win on level or bread.
- **C.LEAD_HOLD: a flag-lead hold from r1500**: When we lead by one at r1500 (216 games, 14%), it erases the lead in 39%, and a tie becomes a level-sum loss (45 of the 66 losses). Its chains convert by local superiority: g0 minus our defenders at the grab of -2 or less gives 6-9%, +4 or more gives 18-31%, and the raw defender count does not matter. With HOLD_ROUND=1500, while our captures exceed its captures and round >= HOLD_ROUND, non-defenders take the nearest own live flag home as field target and stay within dist2 64 of it. They still chase carriers within CHASE_RADIUS2 and still capture with a carried flag. Not the closed recall line: recall reacts to a threat all game, while this is a standing posture conditioned on the score, used only when the lead is decisive under the tiebreak. Signature: Fire: note 'lead' turns only when ahead after r1500. Delivery vs andli, on games ahead by one at r1500: our robots within dist2 64 of our own homes after r1500 at least 2x; its chains with g0 - defenders20 >= +2 down by at least 30%; P(it erases the lead) from 0.389 to at most 0.28. Outcome: win rate in those games from 0.694 to at least 0.78. Games not ahead are identical. Expected: +8 to +10 pp in the 14% of games ahead by one at r1500, about +1 to +1.5 pp overall. It can ship together with LATE_ALLIN as one score-aware endgame switch.
- **C.ESCORT_STUN: stuns beside our own carrier, adopted from andli (low priority)**: andli builds 0.87 stuns per trip within dist2 36 of its own carrier, and these freeze 110 of ours a game; we build 0.15 per trip and freeze 12.7. Our failed carriers die in a median 3 rounds to 3.6 hits, many from chasers stepping in. Freezing chasers for about 4 turns buys steps at a carrier's half speed. The switch adds a mirror of Duck.carrierStun() in the fight branch, before placeCombatTrap. A duck with its action ready, within dist2 13 of our carrier, with an enemy within dist2 20 of that carrier, builds a STUN on a legal tile within dist2 8 of the carrier, choosing the tile nearest the nearest chaser and off the carrier's homeward step, with reserve 0. These stuns take priority over ring and combat stuns. Signature: Fire: escort-stun builds per our trip (B-line count) from 0.15 to at least 0.5. Delivery vs andli: stunVictimsEsc from 12.7 to at least 40 a game; its robots near our carriers (op.chasers20) from 2.49 to at most 2.2; carrierDeaths/postPickups from 0.93 to at most 0.88; longCapRate (op row) from 0.51 to at least 0.56; stun builds near our own flag homes no more than 10% below 126. Expected: Small: at most about +0.05 captures a game. The stratified association is +3.5 +- 1.5 pp on our trips of 6 or more rounds (5.5 a game) and null on andli's own trips. Test it only if a cheap stacking slot is open.
