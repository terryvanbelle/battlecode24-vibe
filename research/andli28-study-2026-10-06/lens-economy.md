# Lens economy

## Fights are lost at a constant rate from the dam drop on, and the dam-drop skirmish itself is about even. The losing comes from the r400+ grind. It does not come from attacking less. (strong)

Evidence: From andli-levels.csv (cumulative metrics every 100 rounds, games that last the whole phase). Our share of all deaths (our kills / (our kills + our deaths)) is 0.375 +- 0.009 in r200-400 (n=197), 0.378 +- 0.005 in r400-1000 (n=185) and 0.381 +- 0.005 in r1000-2000 (n=135). Wins vs losses: 0.44 vs 0.35, 0.42 vs 0.37 and 0.43 vs 0.36. Attacks are near parity: 470 vs 479 per 100 rounds in r200-400 and 426 vs 461 in r400-1000. Deaths are not: 30 vs 19, 52 vs 31 and 59 vs 35 per 100 rounds. Our kills/deaths at r250 are 9.0/10.5. Within 93 map x length-bin cells (census2, 1,520 games), our kills250 minus deaths250 is only +1.1 +- 0.6 higher in wins than in losses. The same cells show deaths -6.4 +- 0.6 per 100 rounds and kills +2.5 +- 0.3 per 100 rounds in wins.

Confound: Within a game, our share of deaths is partly an outcome of being pushed back: 76% of our deaths in losses fall in our half vs 52% in wins. The phase means come from the 200-game subset.

## Our engage rule steps hurt robots into reach far more often than andli28 does, and those step-ins are where we die. Micro.fight() lets any robot at or above RETREAT_HP=300 engage. That threshold ignores the ATTACK upgrade at r600, after which a hit does about 210-250. Two hits kill a 300-500 HP robot. (strong)

Evidence: I wrote a scratch replay analyser (not a repo tool) and ran it on the VM over 120 filler replays (80 losses, 40 wins). A decision event is a ready, unfrozen robot whose nearest enemy is at dist2 5-10 at the end of r-1, i.e. one step from reach. HP 300-699: we step in and strike 39.8% +- 0.3% of such decisions (per-game mean, n=120) vs andli28 15.1% +- 0.2%. After stepping in we die within 3 rounds 0.175, vs 0.053 when we hold (difference +0.122 +- 0.003 per game). For andli28 the same pair is 0.064 vs 0.017. HP >= 700: we step 28% (died 0.049), andli28 21% (died 0.036). Our mid-HP step share is the same in wins and losses (0.394 vs 0.400), so it is a fixed policy trait, not a result of losing. That is about 1,075 mid-HP step-and-strike events per game, followed by up to about 188 of our ~831 deaths per game. HP at end of r-1 for our deaths (199,554 deaths in 240 replays): 36% had >= 300 HP vs 28% of theirs, and 27% had 300-499 HP vs 19%.

Confound: Hold vs step is a selection comparison: robots that step may be in hotter spots, so 0.175 vs 0.053 is an upper bound on the effect. Death counts within 3 rounds can count one death twice across consecutive events. The 300 HP retreat dose (T8, 500/700 on g_iter1) leaned negative, but that ran before HEAL_HOLD and changed retreating, not engaging.

## Our step-ins strike ready enemies. Andli28's strike enemies that are on cooldown or stunned. Their hits therefore land on our robots when those robots cannot strike back. This is the tempo gap that the readyHeld20 column measures. (strong)

Evidence: From 240 replays (754k of our step-ins, 933k of theirs). A step-in = moving to a tile within dist2 4 of an enemy's previous tile from a tile with none. Of our step-in strikes, 44% hit an enemy that was ready at the end of r-1; for andli28 it is 16%. Strikes on stunned targets are 8.5% of our step-ins vs 15.4% of theirs. Our step-in strike kills its target within 2 rounds 22.5% of the time vs their 43.8%. After a step-in we die within 3 rounds 13.8% of the time vs their 5.7%. This holds in every phase: 0.059 vs 0.021 (r201-400), 0.135 vs 0.053 (r401-1000), 0.165 vs 0.071 (r1001+). Hits received (1.74M on us, 1.64M on them): on our robots 18.3% land while ready, 14.8% while frozen, 67.2% while on cooldown. On theirs: 41.1%, 7.5%, 51.7%. Census readyHeld20 is 0.280 +- 0.002 vs 0.402 +- 0.001. healThreat10 is 0.127 vs 0.089, and it does not separate our wins from losses (within cells -0.001 +- 0.001).

Confound: RobotInfo has no cooldown fields, so we cannot copy the exact rule. Andli28's 'step-in without strike' (34% of its step-ins) includes cases where our robot kited away during the round.

## We step into crowds. Fight()'s 'strong' test counts every visible enemy (allies within dist2 20, plus one, against all enemies in vision), so a dense group around the target tile does not stop an engage. (moderate)

Evidence: The mean number of ready enemies within dist2 10 of our step-in tile is 2.30, vs 1.57 for theirs. 40% of our step-ins have 3+ ready threats vs 21% of theirs. For those step-ins our died-within-3 is 0.182 at 3 threats and 0.263 at 4+; the target dies only 0.146 / 0.131, a net-negative trade. When enemies outnumber our supporters by 1, 2 or 3+ (supporters = our allies within dist2 8), our step-ins net -0.07, -0.18 and -0.31 (target killed minus self died). At the decision level our step-and-strike rate rises with enemies within dist2 10 (0.24 at 1, 0.35-0.36 at 2-4), while andli28's falls (0.18, then 0.21, 0.16, 0.11, 0.10). At full HP, stepping into 3+ enemies still pays in the decision data (local kills 0.352 vs died 0.103).

Confound: Threats are counted at the end of the round, after the enemy's reply. Kill credits count every enemy within dist2 10 that dies, so they overlap across our robots.

## The stun gap is volume, not follow-up. Stun freezes explain most of the ready-strike gap, and the volume gap opens after r400. (strong)

Evidence: From 120 replays with the corrected analyser: andli28's stuns freeze 2,286 of our robots a game; ours freeze 924 of theirs. Of our frozen robots, 15.6% die within 4 rounds (baseline for unfrozen robots near an enemy: 7.7%, a 2x lift). Of theirs, 19.1% die (baseline 4.2%, a 4.5x lift). So we already follow up on our stuns at least as well as andli28 does. Robot-rounds with an enemy within dist2 20: 11.1% of ours are frozen vs 4.1% of theirs. Of the ready gap (0.291 vs 0.408), freezes account for about 0.070 and cooldown from earlier actions for 0.056. 18.9% of our deaths were stunned at r-1 vs 12.6% of theirs. Census: enemy stun triggers 32.8 vs our 14.9 per 100 rounds. Our trap triggers fall from 21.9 per 100 rounds (r200-400) to 14.1 (r400-1000) while theirs stay at 31-34. Within map x length cells, losses carry +3.9 +- 0.4 more enemy stuns per 100 rounds.

Confound: The stun economy is a closed line (Cyril, g7bank). Stun counts follow kill rewards, which follow who wins the fight (paid-kill share 0.21 vs 0.53).

## Most of the level-sum deficit, the class of 41% of losses, is heal and attack XP that follows from the fight, not digs. Heal coverage of a damaged robot is at parity. Andli28 heals more because its damaged robots survive while ours die. (moderate)

Evidence: Regression on 188 games of the r200-800 level-gap growth (mean -38.2 levels; R2 0.91). At the means: heal-count difference contributes -27.5 levels, attacks -9.8, traps -3.8, digs -1.5; deaths are not significant. Heal coverage, the share of robot-rounds in which a damaged robot receives a heal: 300-699 HP with an enemy within dist2 10, 17.6% vs 21.4%; at dist2 11-20, 26.1% vs 27.5%; no enemy within 20, 30.2% vs 30.0%. Andli28 spends 45.8% of its robot-rounds at 300-999 HP vs our 32.3%, and 48% at full HP vs our 59%. Its heals run 642 vs 475 per 100 rounds in r400-1000.

Confound: Heals, attacks and deaths are collinear, so the regression is correlation only. The level-sum gap also includes about 13 levels from setup digs and the end dump.

## Several suspects are not where the fights are lost: chasing carriers, lone or trickle deaths, the r250 skirmish. Location matters only as an outcome (lines already closed). (moderate)

Evidence: Only 2.2% of our deaths had an enemy carrier within dist2 20 (2.6% of theirs); 98% of our deaths came on a 'fight' indicator note. The lone-death share is higher in our wins than our losses (0.15-0.21 vs 0.12-0.17 by length bin). Trickle deaths: 49.5 a game vs their 13. spawnDeath10 is 0.099 +- 0.002 vs 0.031 +- 0.001, and 9.6% of our deaths came within 10 rounds of spawning vs 2.8% (g7spawn is closed). 69% of our deaths and 70% of theirs fall in our half. homeDeathShare (share of our deaths on our own territory) is 0.534 vs 0.210, and within cells our wins have it -0.13 +- 0.02 lower.

Confound: Where fights happen and spawn deaths largely follow from losing the fight. Forward drift, territory micro and spawn safety were tested and closed.

## Levers

- **C.ENGAGE_HP (no step-in below two hits of HP)**: In Micro.fight(), the engage branch (score 10000 - th*100) and the advance branch (5000 - minD*20) would also require rc.getHealth() >= C.ENGAGE_HP: 600, with a dose of 500/700, or 2x the visible enemy hit plus a margin once ATTACK is bought. Exception: the tile reaches a target with e.health <= rc.getAttackDamage(), or a flag carrier; the inRange loop gets a kill flag for this. Below the threshold the duck takes the existing kite/hold branch. It still strikes anything that steps into reach (the strike-first step at the top), and it sits in HEAL_HOLD's dist2 11-20 band, where 26% of damaged robot-rounds get healed vs 17.6% inside dist2 10. RETREAT_HP stays at 300. This differs from the closed T8 dose: robots stay in the fight as counter-strikers instead of retreating, and HEAL_HOLD now pays for holding. Signature: These need a census column or the scratch analyser (VM ~/fxscratch, outside the repo; source in the scratchpad at fights/fx2/fx/Fx.java). (1) Ready robots at 300-699 HP one step from an enemy that step in and strike: 0.40 -> <= 0.22 (andli28 0.15). (2) Our deaths with >= 300 HP at r-1: 36% -> <= 30%. (3) Deaths within 3 rounds of a step-in: 0.139 -> <= 0.10. (4) Census readyHeld20: 0.28 -> >= 0.31. (5) Census deaths/100r down >= 8% with kills/100r down no more than 3%. Expected: Upper bound about -130 deaths a game (-16%: 1,075 mid-HP step-ins x (0.175 - 0.053)) for about -0 to -5% kills, so our share of all deaths rises +0.03 to +0.05. Our damaged-but-alive robot-rounds rise, and so do heals and XP, so the r200-800 level gap (-38 levels, 72% from heals) should narrow. That works on the level-sum loss class (41% of losses) as well as on the capture losses.
- **C.ENGAGE_LOCAL (tile-local odds instead of vision-wide 'strong')**: Replace the engage condition (strong || th <= ENGAGE_MAX_THREAT) with a per-tile test. A tile qualifies if th(l) <= C.ENGAGE_LOCAL_MAX (2). Or if th(l) <= the allies within dist2 8 of l. Or if it reaches a killable or flag-carrying target, or (with lever 3) a stunned one. th(l) counts enemies within dist2 10. Today 'strong' compares allies within dist2 20 against every visible enemy, so a crowd around the target tile does not stop the step. Stack it on ENGAGE_HP; on its own it matters less. Signature: (1) Mean ready threats within dist2 10 of our step-in tiles: 2.30 -> <= 1.8 (andli28 1.57). (2) Share of our step-ins with 5+ enemies within dist2 10: 0.24 -> <= 0.15. (3) At the decision level our step-in rate should fall as nearby enemies rise; today it climbs from 0.24 to 0.36, while andli28's falls from 0.18 to 0.10. (4) Deaths within 3 rounds of a step-in fall at an unchanged target-kill rate. Expected: Smaller and less certain than lever 1. At full HP, stepping into 3+ enemies still trades positively in the decision data (local kills 0.35 vs died 0.10). The gain comes mostly from mid-HP crowd step-ins (died 0.273), which lever 1 already removes. Expect about +0.01 to +0.02 on top of lever 1. Read it as a stack arm.
- **C.STUN_PRESS (treat enemies caught in our stun as free targets)**: When Duck.placeCombatTrap() or carrierStun() builds a stun, the builder writes its location and round to a small Comms ring (4-8 slots). Each turn, a robot with a ring tile in vision checks rc.senseMapInfo(loc).getTrapType(); our own traps are visible to us. NONE means the stun fired. The robot then marks enemies within dist2 13 of that tile as frozen for 2 rounds. In fight(), frozen enemies are left out of th, nearEnemies and the 'strong' count. They rank first in bestTarget after flag carriers. A tile that reaches one waives the ENGAGE_HP and ENGAGE_LOCAL gates. Signature: (1) Share of our step-in strikes that hit a stunned target: 0.085 -> >= 0.14 (andli28 0.154; these strikes kill the target 62% of the time and we die after them 6.9%). (2) Share of enemies caught by our stuns that die within 4 rounds: 0.191 -> >= 0.25. (3) Census kills/100r up >= 5% at unchanged stunTrig. Expected: Modest. About +30 to +60 kills a game on our 924 victims a game: our follow-up already lifts their 4-round death rate 4.5x over baseline, so the stun count, not the follow-up, limits the gain. Its main value is as the exception that stops levers 1-2 from passing up their best trades. Bytecode: up to 8 senseMapInfo calls a turn.
