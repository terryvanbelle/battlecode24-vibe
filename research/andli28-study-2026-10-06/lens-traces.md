# Lens traces

## Half of our post-setup stuns are flag-ring rebuilds that work at less than half the rate of our field stuns. Andli builds almost no ring stuns. (strong)

Evidence: 8 traced games (6 losses: Valentine s684331997, Randy s899227766, Alien s293688211, AceOfSpades s71788507, DefaultLarge s1474539861, Rivers s956412907; 2 wins: Puzzle s588145150, DefaultMedium s219708757). Source: replay-dump --trapgeo joined to --from/--to build and trigger events; dumps on the VM in /tmp/andli-trace. A ring stun is one built within dist2 13 of one of our r200 flag homes. Of our 1,631 post-setup stuns, 862 (53%) are ring stuns, 108 a game (range 53-167). Andli: 103 of 4,657 (2%). Victims per stun: ring 2.27 (13% catch nobody), our field stuns 5.45 (4% catch nobody), andli's field stuns 4.31. Median build-to-trigger time: ring 47-158 rounds, field 2-48 (6 of 8 games at or under 24). Cost was estimated from the build level of the nearest robot at build time (the defenders reach build 6). Ring stuns cost about 72 crumbs and 31.6 crumbs per victim. Field stuns cost about 99 crumbs and 18.1 per victim. Rings take about 7,800 crumbs a game, about 45% of our post-setup stun spending. The cause is in the code: defenders are idx 0-2, so they act first every round. They rebuild at 200 crumbs or more (100 + DEF_TRAP_RESERVE 100); a combat stun needs 300 or more (100 + TRAP_RESERVE 200). After r300 our bank was in [200,300) in 63% of 10-round samples and at 300 or more in only 5%. Setup is the same: 229 of our 246 setup traps are ring traps. Census of 1,003 full-length games: the share of our stun victims caught within 10 rounds of the build is 0.41 in losses and 0.44 in wins; andli's is 0.52-0.54.

Confound: Field stuns are built only with 3 or more enemies in sight, so they get more victims by construction. Ring stuns may be worth more per victim, because they catch raiders at the moment of a grab, and the 8 traces cannot show how many flags the rings saved. Cost comes from a nearest-robot proxy. Ring share is 0.30-0.73 in the losses and 0.37-0.46 in the 2 wins: correlation only, n=8.

## Our hits turn into kills at half andli's rate, because we spread damage and they heal it. This is the clearest difference between our losses and our wins. (strong)

Evidence: Every attack, heal, spawn and death after setup in the 8 traces (about 97k hits). A damage episode is a run of hits on one target with gaps of 5 rounds or less. r201-450: our episodes end in a kill 10.2% of the time (3,999 episodes), andli's 20.7% (3,402). From r451: 19.5% vs 37.8% (19,261 vs 16,510). Hits needed for a kill are the same (about 4-5). Our episodes are shorter: 2.5 hits vs 3.0, single-hit episodes 37% vs 30%, distinct attackers 1.8-2.0 vs 2.0-2.8. Attacks per kill in r201-450: ours 37, 17, 27, 23, 32, 57 in the 6 losses (W1 20, W2 14); andli's 12-16 (W2 22). We are lower in 7 of 8 games early (only W2 reverses) and in 8 of 8 later. Target HP was reconstructed (spawn 1000, -150 or -210 per hit, +80 or +130 per heal). Hits on targets at 450 HP or less: ours 21.6%, andli's 35.3% in r201-450 (10,047 vs 10,342 hits); 19.0% vs 26.8% later. In W2 the gap nearly closes (0.30 vs 0.33). Typical sequence (DefaultLarge r280-330): an andli robot takes 4 hits in r290-291, then 9 heals, and is never finished. Heals per attack: andli 1.10-1.43, ours 0.88-1.07. Code: Micro.fight's engage branch picks the reaching tile with the fewest threats (10000 - th*100 + adj*10) and ignores which enemy it reaches. Strike-first hits whatever is in range before moving.

Confound: Part of the gap may be caused by their stuns: our frozen attackers cannot follow up, and our frozen wounded cannot escape. 39% of our deaths follow an enemy stun within 4 rounds and dist2 13; for their deaths the figure is 29%. The HP model ignores level bonuses. Only 8 games.

## Most of the level-sum deficit is heal levels, not build or attack levels. (strong)

Evidence: replay-dump --levels at r2000 on random samples (VM): 30 level-sum losses, 15 more-flags losses, 15 full-length wins. Level-sum losses: heal -32.2 ± 3.6, build -5.4 ± 2.2, attack -4.5 ± 2.0 (total -42.0, median -34). More-flags losses: heal -36.1 ± 3.6, build -12.9 ± 2.8, attack -12.5 ± 3.1. Wins: heal -26.7 ± 4.5, attack +11.9 ± 2.4, build +10.9 ± 4.7. Andli has 24-44 heal masters (heal level 4+) to our 7-20. Andli's three robots at attack 3, build 6, heal 3 only match our own build-6 defenders. Heal volume in traced games: andli 10,454 vs our 8,221 (Valentine), 12,406 vs 8,621 (Randy). Engine (InternalRobot.jailedPenalty): a death costs XP only from the highest skill (heal -10 at levels 3-4, -15 at 5), and we die 1.6-2x as often. Wasted hits explain the mirror: about 69% of our hits land on robots that survive, and those hits become andli's heals. Over 237 games, about a third of the end gap already exists at r400 (losses -28.0, wins -15.7; difference 12.4 ± 2.7).

Confound: Levels at r2000 come from 60 sampled games. Heal volume follows from how many robots are alive and how much damage each side takes. That is an outcome of the fights, not a separate policy.

## The first post-dam fight (r200-400) mostly decides the game. In losses our army collapses in a short window and the fight then stays in our half. (moderate)

Evidence: andli-levels.csv (237 games with r200 and r400 rows): our deaths minus theirs over r200-400 average +26.7 in losses and +10.2 in wins (difference 16.5 ± 3.3). Win rate by tercile of that gap: 0.43, 0.29, 0.11. Heals over r200-400 (ours minus theirs): -252 in losses, -133 in wins (difference 119 ± 22). Traces: every one of the 6 losses has a 20-round window in r330-430 with 13-18 of our deaths to 0-2 of theirs. W1 has one too, W2 none. DefaultLarge r297-364: 50 of our deaths to 2 of theirs while our line fell back from x=43 to x=13 on a 60-wide map. Across those 120 rounds (r280-400) andli out-healed us about 2:1 (976 vs 493) at par attacks. Boards (--map-at, 14 rounds per game): from r400 to r1600, 0-6 of our robots are in andli's half in most snapshots of DefaultLarge, Rivers, Alien and Valentine, and 20-45 of theirs are in ours. In W2, 13-35 of ours are in their half from r250 to r500. In r201-450, our deaths in andli's half or near its spawn total 306 to their 81 across the 6 losses (3.8:1), and 288 to 193 in our half. W2: 36 of ours to 76 of theirs in their half.

Confound: Map and side confound the r400 split (correlation only). The collapse follows andli's stun burst: its traps go +53 to +98 in r250-400 against our +15 to +19, funded by a bank of about 4,800 crumbs at r250. Forward drift and zone holds are already closed lines.

## Andli's stuns come from three build-6 builders and are built in front of our robots. (moderate)

Evidence: --trapgeo: 99-100% of andli's post-setup stuns are built with one of our robots within dist2 20, against 30-72% of ours. Median build-to-trigger time 9-15 rounds. In about 60% of its stuns the nearest andli robot is at build level 6, and every traced game has exactly three of them (attack 3, build 6, heal 3: its ~80-90 setup digs at about 30 each). A cheapest-tier stun costs about 50 crumbs. Example of a trail (DefaultLarge r251-292): one raider built 28 stuns about a tile apart along y=0-2 on its way to our flag at (0,0). Our robots then walked the line and triggered 12 of them in sequence; robot #12387 alone triggered 8 in r297-345.

Confound: Nearest-robot build level is a proxy for the builder. Builders and front stuns are closed lines; this is context, not a new lever. As a share, we re-trigger stuns no more than andli does (29% vs 31% within 12 rounds), so trail-walking is a volume effect.

## Several suspected weaknesses of ours did not show up in the traces. (moderate)

Evidence: (a) Spawn waves: 66% of our spawns already come 4 or more within 3 rounds and 4 tiles. The 20-round death rate is the same for solo and batched spawns (19% of 469 vs 18% of 4,597), so a spawn wave has no premise. (b) Depth: from boards at r300-1400, the share of robots in front, support and far bands is about equal (ours 0.55-0.77 in front, andli's 0.55-0.69). (c) Local odds: fight turns where we see at least 3 more allies than enemies carry 36% of our deaths on 69% of fight turns, so advancing with numbers is not where we die. Even or outnumbered turns carry 64% of deaths on 31% of turns. (d) Heal hold: 36% of held-heal turns are followed by an attack next round and 55% within 3 rounds, the same in wins and losses. (e) Our robots in fights see 7-11 allies to 3.5-4.5 enemies. Local numbers are not the deficit.

Confound: 8 games. Held turns also count turns with no damaged ally in reach.

## Andli's captures come from long trips nobody chases. Its short tries at our flags die. Recall and intercept are closed, so this is context only. (weak)

Evidence: --defense: the captures took 54 and 76 rounds with 0.1 mean chasers within dist2 20 (Alien r1215, r1261), 82 rounds with 1.0 (DefaultLarge r1571), and 39 and 48 rounds with 2.2 and 0.0 (Rivers r1535, r1551). Meanwhile 20-35 short tries a game died with 7-21 of ours near. After our own capture at r287 on Alien, we made no pickup for 1,160 rounds. Over 1,003 full-length games, 30-34% of loss games have no pickup by us after r600.

Confound: Closed lines (recall, contact or convoy, relay). No new lever is claimed.

## The heal hold turned our heal lead over andli at r400 into a deficit. No change in the level gap or win rate is detectable. (weak)

Evidence: Band surveys of andli games: g_iter6 heal400 1,293-1,350 against andli's 1,172-1,203 (36 games). g6heal and g_iter7: 915-981 against 1,156-1,265 (48 games). Win rate 28% vs 29%. g_iter7 turned capture losses into long games (capture-all losses 15 to 6 of 36).

Confound: Small n, different blocks: correlation only. Heal levels make up most of the level-sum gap (finding 3), so watch any further hold dose.

## Levers

- **RING_POST: after setup, rebuild flag rings only under threat**: In Duck.defend(), when G.round > SETUP_ROUNDS, a new C.RING_POST_THREAT (boolean, default false = g_iter7) allows a ring trap only when an enemy is within dist2 20 of the defender. Dose alternative: a post-setup ring reserve of 400 in place of DEF_TRAP_RESERVE 100, so the ring gate (500) sits above the combat-stun gate (300). Defenders act first each round and today take every crumb above 200 while a ring tile is empty. That costs about 7,800 crumbs a game on stuns that catch 2.27 enemies after about 100 rounds. Field stuns catch 5.45 within 2-16 rounds. The change also moves build XP from defenders already at build level 6 (wasted beyond 30 XP; they lose 6 XP per death) to field robots, who lose no build XP on death. Signature: New replay counts (a 2-column ReplayDump addition, or the ev/trapgeo join used here): post-setup stuns within dist2 13 of our flag homes from about 108 to 45 or fewer a game; their median trigger time from about 100 rounds to 20 or fewer; field stuns from about 96 to 140 or more a game. Existing census columns: stunVictimsFast/stunVictims from 0.41-0.44 to 0.50 or more; stunVictims +20%. Guards: enemyCaptured and enemyFirstGrabs not up more than 10%; captured600 / enemyCaptured600 unchanged. Expected: About +150-250 enemy robots frozen by our stuns a game (+20-30%), concentrated in the r250-400 fight that decides the game. About +5-10 build levels at r2000 for the level-sum class. The main risk is raids getting through at undefended rings, so the delivery gate needs the captures guard.
- **FOCUS: finish the wounded**: New C.FOCUS_STEP (default false) in Micro.fight. (1) Engage branch: each reaching tile gets +(1000 - lowest HP among enemies within dist2 4 of the tile)/C.FOCUS_W (default 10, so at most +100, about one threat). It gets a further +C.KILL_BONUS (150) if that HP is at or below rc.getAttackDamage(). (2) Strike-first: if no enemy in range has HP at or below our damage, one within dist2 8 does, and movement is ready, step to the tile that reaches it and then strike. Today the tile is chosen by fewest threats, whichever enemy it reaches, and strike-first hits whatever is in range. Wounded andli robots step back and are healed: 37% of our damage episodes are a single hit, and 69-79% of our hits land on robots that survive. This is one term in two branches, not a rescored micro. MICRO_V2, a full rewrite that also had a kill bonus, lost 1-25 in iteration 7. Signature: New ReplayDump columns using the replay's per-robot HP: hitLowShare (our hits on enemies at 450 HP or less) in r201-450 from 0.22 to 0.30 or more; episodeKillRate (target hit with gaps of 5 rounds or less, ending in its death) in r201-450 from 0.10 to 0.15 or more, and from r451 from 0.195 to 0.25 or more; distinct attackers per episode from 1.85 to 2.1 or more; attacks per kill in r201-450 from about 29 to 20 or fewer. Downstream in existing data: andli heals per our attack falls (now 1.10-1.43); in andli-levels-style metrics, our deaths minus theirs over r200-400 falls from +26.7 in losses; kills250, deaths250 and k/d improve. Expected: Closing even a third of the conversion gap would cut the r200-400 death gap by about 8-10. That gap predicts outcome: win rate 0.43, 0.29, 0.11 by tercile. Fewer wasted hits also means less andli heal XP, and heal levels are 77% of the level-sum deficit, so a level-gap gain of roughly 5-15 is plausible. This is the lever with the widest reach: it touches capture, more-flags and level-sum losses.

Metrics: our traps built r201-400 +30%; deaths minus kills over r200-400 falls. Guard: enemyCaptured600 not up more than 10%. In LS2, CA1 and CA2, 50-85% of setup ring stuns fired by r400 during early raids, so raids may get further. Expected: Small to moderate, and uncertain.
