# Critic

- **C.FINAL_COMPLETE (finish partial build levels in r1950-2000, no bank)**: keep=True, rank 1. Survives, but the gain is smaller and less certain than claimed. CONFIRMED from census2 (n=1,520): 410 level-sum losses, median deficit 42; 28/33/37/46/66 within 10/12/13/15/20 levels. The near misses cluster on one map: EndAround holds 9 of the 33 within 12 and 11 of the 46 within 15. The study picks 5(a) maps to match, which is fine, but a random-map gain will be lower than the EndAround diagnostic suggests. Levels file (59 level-sum losses of 200 games): our bank is 244 at r1900 and 241 at r2000; we build ~13 traps per 100 rounds late and dig 0; andli28 gains +12.6 levels in r1900-2000 (87 -> 209 digs) to our +5.7. So the budget is income-bound at ~600-800 crumbs. Availability is not the limit: VM --logs on 3 near-miss losses (Rivers, MazeRunner, EndAround) show 19-45% of our robot-turns in r1950-2000 in the fight branch, 6% defending, the rest free (jailed included). NOT VERIFIED: the stranded-XP figures (6.0/13.8/23.7 robots within 1/2/3 digs; 113 of 251 XP stranded) come from a scratch XP dump not in the data files. Since the 59 level losses average 35 alive robots, roughly 30% of the 50 are jailed at any time. Precedents: the same family delivered +7 (g7bank, up to 53 digs, n=7 tied games) and +8 (g4farm2 vs Gymhgy) but never shipped. Partial targeting (about 1.6-2.7 digs per level against g7bank's ~5-7) is a real mechanistic difference, so this does not repeat the closed g7bank line. Errors in the case: (1) 'Flag state frozen late' is overstated. In the 200-game subset, 10 of 69 games tied at r1900 saw a capture afterwards (andli28 7, us 3), not 2+2 of 63. (2) The crumb gate lets the bank fall to ~0 (FC_KEEP[1]=0), and then the 'kept' carrier stuns cannot be paid. Add a 100-crumb floor for carrier and alert stuns. (3) The :78 hook sits before tryHeal, so digs replace heals; that is harmless for levels but should be logged. (4) The 5(a) bar (c) of +10 sits at the realistic mean (+8 to +14). A working mechanism may still miss it, so +8 with the falsifier at +5 is fairer. Corrected expectation: +1.2 to +2.2 pp, P(delivers >= +8 levels) about 0.6. It remains the best lever. Bytecode is trivial: an 8-tile canDig scan.
- **C.ENGAGE_HP (no step-in below ~600 HP unless it kills; hold at dist2 11-20)**: keep=True, rank 2. Survives as a weaker #2. Its novelty claim and gain estimate are overstated. CONFIRMED: kill share 0.369 pooled (505 vs 862 per game); wins above losses in each length bin: <r800 0.515+-0.020 vs 0.369+-0.015 (n=87), r800-1999 0.411+-0.005 vs 0.335+-0.003 (n=430), r2000 0.429+-0.003 vs 0.364+-0.002 (n=1,003). Within-map slope 2.55 (2.57 within map x side, 2.48 within map x length). readyHeld20 0.280 vs 0.402. In level-sum games the level gap tracks deaths-kills (r -0.856, -0.131 levels per net death, n=461). PROBLEMS: (1) It is not clearly distinct from the T8 ladder. RETREAT_HP 500/700 already applied this lever's gate (via 'hurt': no engage and no advance below the threshold) plus a retreat, and it went the wrong way: 82/110 (4 gained, 6 lost) and 80/110 (3, 7) vs 84. That was weak, in an old era, against the field, but it is the only direct experiment. (2) The +2 to +4 pp comes from halving a correlational slope, which has no basis. The slope is mostly outcome. Within map, kill share correlates +0.65 with paidKillShare and -0.65 with homeDeathShare (winning moves the fight). andli28's own readyHeld20 correlates +0.21 with OUR kill share, so readyHeld20 is partly an outcome too and a poor delivery signature; use the Fx step-in share. The least outcome-driven proxy, the dam-drop skirmish (kills250-deaths250, within map), gives only ~3.6 pp of win per SD (6.2 net kills). (3) A better gain estimate goes through the level channel: net -30 to -50 deaths x 0.131 = +4 to +7 levels, about 11-21 level-sum flips, +0.7 to +1.4 pp, plus an unknown flag-class effect. (4) The step-vs-hold death rates are selection-biased (andli28's ratio 3.8x vs ours 3.3x). The 36% high-HP deaths are probably stun follow-up (andli28 freezes 2,320 of ours per game to our 917), which this lever does not touch. Part of its benefit would come through stepping onto fewer trapped tiles, which overlaps the parked stun-wary line. The step-in shares (39.8% vs 15.1%) come from a scratch analyser not in the data files. Corrected: +0.7 to +2 pp, P about 0.25. Bytecode: one comparison, fine.
- **C.RING_POST_RESERVE (post-setup ring rebuilds yield crumbs to field stuns)**: keep=True, rank 3. Kept at low priority; its risk is under-weighted. CONFIRMED from census2: trapsBuilt 265 vs 576, stunTrig 225 vs 513, stunVictims 917 vs 2,320, fast-victim share 0.409 vs 0.537. Selection is less of a worry than the study fears: STUN_ENEMIES_MIN=3, and the marginal field stun is bought in the same >=3-enemy situations that the 300-crumb gate now blocks. PROBLEMS: (1) It overlaps tested arms. c5bank (flag rings only above a 1,000 bank) and c6pair (plus combat stuns from 1 enemy) were both 82/110 vs 84, though both were 'starved' (bank gone by r250, never delivered), so that evidence is weak. The Cyril stun-economy arms were neutral, and g4front failed delivery because stuns were crumb-bound. (2) Flag risk is under-weighted. Flag losses are 661 of 1,071 losses; andli28 makes 10.7 first grabs a game with 4.2 of ours near at the grab and captures 1.39 a game. The 5(a) guard (sum of enemyCaptured <= twins + 2 over 12 games, baseline ~17, SD ~4) cannot detect a 15% rise; pool 24+ games or bar on enemyFirstGrabs per game. (3) There is a level-sum side effect it does not budget. 108 ring builds a game give the 3 defenders up to 30 build XP each (up to 18 levels), and moving that XP to scattered fighters strands more in partials. Sign unknown; measure the defenders' build levels before the 5(a), since the level class is 41% of losses. The ring-vs-field yields (2.27 vs 5.45 victims) come from 8 traced games only. Corrected: +0.5 to +1.5 pp, P about 0.2.
- **C.FOCUS_STEP (engage tile scored by target HP and kill)**: keep=True, rank 4. Weakest kept lever. Code check: bestTarget (Micro.java:35-41) already strikes the lowest-HP enemy in reach, so the lever only changes which reaching tile is chosen. The study never measures how often a ready step-in has tiles reaching different enemies with different HP; with 3.5-4.5 visible enemies, few choices will differ. Its evidence is confounded by target supply. andli28 hits <=450-HP targets 35% vs our 22% because we offer them: we step in at 300-699 HP 40% of the time vs its 15% (the ENGAGE_HP finding). A focus tile cannot create low-HP andli28 robots in reach. The kill-within-2-rounds gap (22.5% vs 43.8%) is not a stun effect: removing the stunned-target strikes (62% kill) leaves about 18.8% vs 40.5%. It remains a numbers or follow-up gap (andli28 holds a ready strike 0.40), which a per-robot tile bonus addresses only indirectly. MICRO_V2 contained the same kill-tile term (V2_KILL=60) and lost 1-25. That loss came with a full rewrite, so it is weak evidence, but it is the only test. The modified strike-first (skip a sure hit to step toward a killable enemy at dist2 5-8) adds threat exposure that ENGAGE_HP is trying to remove; test it separately or drop it. Both lenses' figures (97k hits over 8 games; 240 replays) come from scratch analysers not in the data files. Corrected: +0.3 to +1 pp, P about 0.15. Bytecode is fine (~2k).
- **C.LATE_ALLIN (score-aware assault when tied or behind from r1800)**: keep=False, rank 5. Refuted on its key premise and too close to the closed offence lines. In the 200-game subset (andli-levels.csv), 75 games were tied at r1800 (37.5%, matching the lens's 35%) and we won 9 (12%, the lens says 17.8%; SE 3.8). After r1800, andli28 captured in 17 of those 75 games (23%) and we captured in 6 (8%). The lens's line that 'a capture by andli28 only turns a likely level loss into a flag loss' ignores the offset: our extra capture is a win only if andli28 does not answer, and the lever pulls non-defenders off alerts, which should raise andli28's late conversion that is already 3x ours. Behind-by-one games: 34 in the subset, 1 won (2.9%). A rough net: P(we capture) 0.08 -> 0.17 and P(they capture) 0.23 -> 0.28 gives wins among tied games about 0.11 -> 0.15, +1.4 pp overall, before the level cost of deaths (0.131 levels per net death; 14 of our 51 level-sum wins have margins <= 9). The 'bigger grab groups convert' evidence is correlational: g0 >= 8 happens when we already hold the local fight. Presence at andli28's spawn-centre flags is exactly what forward drift (g1drift), convoy dive, zone holds and ADVANCE_MARGIN 3 -> 1 (e1aggr, flat) failed to deliver; a time gate does not change the mechanism. P about 0.15, EV about 0.2 pp; drop it, or retest only after ENGAGE_HP changes the fight trade.

## Missing

1) Basics hole under rule 15, larger than the study says. In census2, symOk=0 in 36 games (Soccer 15/16, Gauntlet 14/17, Waterworld 6, Puzzle 1), and we won 2 of 36 against 30% elsewhere. 19 games still had symmetry undecided after r400 and won 0. That is up to about +0.6 pp (correlation; those maps may be hard anyway), and it is an absolute bar. The vertical-symmetry penalty (0.195 vs 0.366) should be re-run without these 36 games before anyone traces it.
2) Late flag churn was not checked on the full set. In the 200-game subset, 10 of 69 games tied at r1900 saw a capture afterwards (andli28 7, us 3), not 2+2 of 63. That affects FINAL_COMPLETE's crumb-floor risk and kills LATE_ALLIN's premise.
3) The stranded build XP behind FINAL_COMPLETE (6.0/13.8/23.7 robots within 1/2/3 digs) is not in the shared files. Run one --levels/XP dump at r1950 on ~10 level losses on the VM to confirm it before the 5(a). Also count how many robots hold attack or heal 4, since that caps build at 3.
4) No causal estimate exists for kill share. The 2.55 slope is mostly outcome: within map, paidKillShare correlates +0.65 with kill share, and andli28's readyHeld20 correlates +0.21 with ours. Use the level channel (-0.131 levels per net death, r -0.856, n=461) for micro levers.
5) Overlap with earlier experiments was not checked: the T8 RETREAT_HP 500/700 ladder (wrong direction) for ENGAGE_HP, MICRO_V2's V2_KILL for FOCUS, c5bank/c6pair for RING.
6) Power. The 12-cell 5(a) capture guards (+2 on a ~17-capture baseline) cannot detect a 15% rise; flag losses are 62% of losses.
7) Not quantified: how often a step-in has distinct target choices (FOCUS's reach); how much defenders' ring XP adds to the level sum (RING); and whether the late dig hook's lost heals cost anything.
8) There is no g_iter6-vs-andli28 census with the same columns, so HEAL_HOLD's effect on kill share in this matchup is unknown. Any micro lever's baseline shift is unidentified.
