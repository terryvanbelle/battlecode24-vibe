# Lens economy

## Positioning explains only a small part of the upper-tier gap. Most of it is how lethal contact is. At equal local numbers our robots in contact die at 1.6-2.2 times the upper tier's rate. (strong)

Evidence: Scratch analyser Lx over all 720 g_iter7 control replays (~16.8k contact robot-rounds a game vs the upper tier). Contact = an enemy within dist2 10. Local balance b = allies within dist2 20 + 1 - enemies within dist2 20. 2-round death hazard: ours 0.084 vs theirs 0.047 against the upper tier. At equal b ours is far higher: b=+2 0.082 vs 0.040, b=+4 0.064 vs 0.029. This holds in our upper-tier wins too: b=+2 0.066 vs 0.041. Mean b: UP-W +3.27 vs +3.23 (equal), UP-L +3.39 vs +3.93. Putting our hazard curve on their mix of b closes 17% of the gap (20% in losses, -13% in wins). Against the rest, b explains 39% of our edge (our 0.049 vs their 0.083). The hazard falls by about 0.012-0.02 per +1 b for both teams. Scratch tools (not committed): /tmp/claude-1000/-home-terryvanbelle-projects-vibe-2024/0c12d742-3a89-49d0-8e9e-654c983bb00e/scratchpad/pos/{Px,Dx,Rx,Sx,Hx,Vx,Jx,Jx2,Kx,Lx}.java plus their aggregators (*.py); per-game outputs on the VM under ~/pxscratch/out*/.

Confound: Local balance and hazard are both outcomes of the same fights. Positions are end-of-round. This is a 2-round window, and the decomposition is a reweighting, not a causal estimate.

## Against the upper tier, per-contact lethality is mostly stun volume. Their stuns freeze our robots 1.8-3.2 times as often per robot-round, and about half of our deaths come within 5 rounds of a freeze. (strong)

Evidence: Scratch Vx, 720 games. Freeze rate per 100 robot-rounds in contact: ours 6.2/8.5 vs theirs 3.2/2.7 (UP-W/UP-L). The kite band (dist2 11-20) and the staging band (dist2 21-64) show the same pattern. Confound: A death within 5 rounds of a freeze is a time association, not attribution: the same fights produce both freezes and deaths.

## Code defect (rule 15): wounded robots stop at exactly dist2 20 from the enemy. That is the edge of the enemy's vision, still inside it. The stakes are small. (strong)

Evidence: Scratch Kx, 720 games. Among robots under 300 HP in the dist2 11-20 band, 33.5-36% of ours sit at exactly dist2 20 vs 12-15.5% of theirs. Ratio at dist2 20 to dist2 21-25: ours 2.27-2.37, theirs 0.97-1.04; for our healthy robots 0.90. Code: the kite score in src/bot/Micro.java:278 charges -200 only for minD < 20 while the +50 band bonus covers 11-20, so a wounded robot's best tile is exactly 20. Death within 5 rounds for wounded robots at dist2 20 vs 21-40: ours 4.0% vs 2.8% (upper) and 3.2% vs 2.4% (rest). About 617 (upper) and 438 (rest) of our wounded robot-rounds a game sit at dist2 20.

Confound: The death-rate difference is correlational: robots stopped at 20 may be the ones still near a fight.

## Our wounded robots in contact are healed less often than every group of opponents' robots. The cause is readiness and HEAL_HOLD, not where our healers stand. No positional heal lever. (moderate)

Evidence: Scratch Hx, 720 games. Share of wounded contact robots healed the next round: 0.17-0.18 vs 0.20-0.25 (all groups). Of our heals on contact targets, 78-79% already come from a healer outside dist2 10 of any enemy (upper tier 67-69%). Our wounded contact robots have a healer-in-place more often: an ally within dist2 4 that is outside dist2 10 of all enemies, 0.48-0.51 vs 0.44-0.49. But that ally has a ready action less often vs the upper tier (0.067-0.070 vs 0.091-0.099). When a ready ally of that kind exists, the heal rate is equal vs the upper tier (0.37-0.39 for both).

Confound: Readiness is read from end-of-round cooldowns. HEAL_HOLD keeps strikes ready by design (TACTICS T16).

## Differences in how long robots stay in enemy vision are a matter of style, not strength. (moderate)

Evidence: Scratch Rx. Our vision episodes run 0.6-1.5 rounds longer (8 of 8 cells), we leave vision with 15-140 less HP, and their 300-999 HP robots use the dist2 21-64 band more. Across the 20 bots, none of these, nor the share of contact robots healed, correlates with the opponent's win rate against us (r -0.02 to 0.00).

Confound: Only 20 bots, and the comparison is across opponents. Pulling robots back is the closed retreat line.
