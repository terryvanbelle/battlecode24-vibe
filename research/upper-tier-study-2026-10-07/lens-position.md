# Lens position

## Upgrades offer no lever. Every bot in the band, the upper tier included, buys the same upgrades at the same rounds as we do, and every alternative order we tried has already lost. (strong)

Evidence: GLOBAL_UPGRADE strings from the survey upgrades column, 720 control games. Every upper-tier bot buys ATK@600, then HEAL@1200, then CAP@1800, for example andli28 in 28 of 36 games (the rest end before r1800) and jmerle in 18. We do the same: ATK+HEAL+CAP in 255 games, a prefix of it in the rest. Only kyleezz and SampleProvider (rest of band) buy CAP second. History: heal-first 22-26, capture-first 14-33 (p~0.005), capture-second g2up3 delivery FAIL (kills -6.6%, -2.9 SE). Points arrive at fixed rounds, cost nothing and are bought at once, so there is no timing to choose.

Confound: None material. We cannot see the enemy bank, so we cannot tell why they buy as they do.

## Collecting more map crumbs is not a lever. About 98% of map crumbs are collected, and our share against the upper tier is about equal (48%). (strong)

Evidence: A scratch --econ analyser (VM ~/scratch-econ7, not committed) reads the crumbs left by flood-fill territory over the 720 games. Each half starts with 4,371 crumbs; 94 of ours are left at r200, 164 of the upper tier's. The centre holds about 6,200 crumbs and 661 are left at r250. Per game, 255-322 of about 15,000 crumbs are never collected (map totals from the replay summary). Census gatheredAll: 7,180 for us vs 7,457 for the upper tier, 7,883 vs 6,881 against the rest. Setup gathering is equal (4,280 vs 4,210). After the dam opens we trail slightly (gathered201to400 2,800 vs 3,170).

Confound: The r201-400 shortfall follows who wins the dam-drop fight (kills250 9.1 vs 13.4 against the upper tier).

## Against the upper tier, the income gap is the +30 kill reward. It follows where the fight happens, and re-assigning killing blows cannot recover it. (strong)

Evidence: XpDump income residual (kill reward = income minus passive and gathered): per 100 rounds, us 176+-14 vs upper tier 989+-46. Against the four bots that beat us most (chenyx512, andli28, kuma, Strequals) it is 148 vs 942; in r2000 games 3,148 vs 16,115 crumbs. Share of kills that pay, by phase (r201-599 / 600-1199 / 1200-1799 / 1800+): theirs 0.47 / 0.58 / 0.68 / 0.69, ours 0.20 / 0.17 / 0.16 / 0.16 (n 180/168/139/89). Against the rest it reverses: 838 vs 301. Re-assignment: an unpaid kill of ours where another of our robots stood on enemy territory within dist2 4 of the victim is only 1.2% / 0.8% / 0.6% / 0.6% of our kills (+-0.1%). Killer attribution matches census kills (699 plus 20 trap kills vs 720 in the test game).

Confound: Paid share is an outcome of the fight being in our half (64-72% of our deaths). The lines that move the fight (forward drift, territory-aware micro) are closed.

## The upper tier's other economic edge is price. The four bots that beat us 69-83% each make about three build-6 specialists in setup, who then build most of their traps at about half price. Price explains about half of their 2.5x lead in stun count. (strong)

Evidence: XpDump post-setup stun price (crumbs / stuns): those four bots 53.7+-0.5 vs ours 87.8+-0.6 (jmerle 96.0, Cyril 60, Gymhgy 81, the rest 94-99). Their post-setup explosives cost 101 vs our 147. Template: 64-90 setup digs; 2.1-3.0 robots at build >= 4 at r200 and 3.0 at r1000; at r2000 their top three robots hold 412-646 of 552-799 build XP. That means each of those three robots made about 140-215 builds. It is absent in 14 bots, including jmerle (0 digs). Volume: stuns per 100 rounds 36.2+-1.1 vs 14.3+-0.8 and stun victims 155 vs 69, while their trap crumbs per 100 rounds are only 1.17x ours (2,090 vs 1,790). In log terms price explains about 50% of the stun gap and crumbs (kill reward) about 35%. In our 39 wins against these four, their price is unchanged (53.6 vs 53.7 in losses) and they still out-stun us 30.1 vs 18.1 per 100 rounds. Our builds are spread out: our top three are the defenders (~30 build XP each at r2000). Beyond them, 0.2 robots per upper-tier game reach 15 build XP. In --trapgeo, 55% of our post-setup stuns have a build-0 robot as the nearest robot of ours.

Confound: This is a cross-section. Cyril has builders and still loses to us 72%; jmerle has none and beats us 69%. Our builder arms belong to a closed family: g4builder and g4builder2 had five designated builders and drained the bank with setup digs, and g4econ2 was band-neutral (capture -0.08, upper -0.20, kills -17% from the attack/heal stop and crumb detours). How much of the gap is causal is unknown.

## A stun's yield depends strongly on how many enemies are near the trap tile when it is built. We are crumb-bound, so a fifth of our field stuns spend scarce crumbs on the lowest-yield class. (strong)

Evidence: --trapgeo, 72 games against upper and mid bots (48 against the top four; about 13k of our field stuns, i.e. an enemy within dist2 20 at build). Victims per built stun, by enemies within dist2 13 of the tile at build: 0-2: 3.36+-0.06, 3-4: 4.31+-0.04, 5+: 6.93+-0.05. Median trigger latency 36 / 10 / 3 rounds; share triggered within 10 rounds 0.27 / 0.48 / 0.70. Class shares 0.21 / 0.39 / 0.40. Rest-of-band sample (36 games, 34 won): 2.83 / 3.78 / 6.16, shares 0.20 / 0.48 / 0.32, so the pattern holds band-wide. Crumb-bound: XpDump shows our bank at 195-290 at every checkpoint from r250 to r2000 in both tiers. That is below placeCombatTrap's 300 gate, so each stun is built on the first chance after the bank refills. Upper bound if the crumbs moved to big groups: +11% victims per crumb if the 0-2 class is dropped, +36% if 0-4 is.

Confound: Enemies near the tile also measures how hot the fight is. Holding crumbs costs stuns in small skirmishes. g4front, which required at least 3 enemies within dist2 13 but also changed placement and reserve, left total victims unchanged against Cyril. ct1/ct2 lowered the threshold and leaned negative (20-22, 16-18), which fits the same direction.

## How we spend crumbs differs from the upper tier mostly on lines already closed. Fills are not a sink compared with them. (moderate)

Evidence: Setup spend: 4,275 crumbs (9.6 explosives = 1,855, at 97-133 crumbs per victim against 21-34 for stuns; 25.6 stuns = 2,420). The upper tier spends 1,050 on digs and banks 3,474. Fills per game: 3,531 for us vs 4,135 for opponents; on water maps (Waterworld 13.3k vs 12.3k) we are not worse. Explosives in r2000 games: 3,610 vs 2,063 crumbs. Post-setup ring rebuilds take about half of our post-setup trap crumbs (andli28 study). Those lines are closed: rings, dam-line budget, setup digs.

Confound: Ring explosives sit on flag-adjacent tiles. Their deterrence is not counted in crumbs per victim.

## Our level gap to the upper tier comes from deaths (jail XP) and less heal XP gained. It does not come from an economic policy, and level-sum deficits are too large for cheap fixes. (strong)

Evidence: XpDump, the 71 upper-tier games that reach r2000. Level sum 348 vs 390: attack -11, build -9, heal -22. We gain more attack XP (5,928 vs 5,180) but lose 2,670 vs 1,528 to jail. Levels lost to jail 292 vs 145, i.e. 0.31 vs 0.24 per death, from 930 vs 598 deaths. Heal XP gained 7,201 vs 8,285, with equal jail loss (2,244 vs 2,411). Masters at r2000: attack-4+ robots 11 vs 14, heal-4+ 16 vs 25. Against the rest we lead the level sum by 20 at r2000. Level sum decides 82 of 720 games; only 6 of 22 upper-tier level-sum losses are within 21 levels, and 5 of 25 rest-of-band losses within 12.

Confound: Heal XP needs robots that are damaged but alive, and C.HEAL_HOLD trades heals for tempo on purpose.

## The upgrade phases do not shift the heal-or-strike trade. After C.HEAL_HOLD our ready-strike share matches theirs, so an upgrade-phase heal rule has no support. (moderate)

Evidence: --econ analyser by phase (r201-599 / 600-1199 / 1200-1799 / 1800+, after ATTACK at r600, HEALING at r1200, CAPTURING at r1800). Heals with an enemy within dist2 10: ours 0.128 / 0.119 / 0.137 / 0.137, upper tier 0.240 / 0.210 / 0.248 / 0.238, flat across phases. Ready strike held near an enemy: 0.261 vs 0.265 and 0.309 vs 0.306, then 0.326 vs 0.370 and 0.344 vs 0.373 (SE 0.005-0.014). Against the rest we lead in every phase (0.30-0.43 vs 0.24-0.37).

Confound: The later phases are a survivor sample (n 180, 168, 139, 89 games).

## The upper tier carries a large bank through the middle and late game; we sit at the build gate. They are not crumb-bound and we are, so any gain in yield or price turns straight into our stuns. (moderate)

Evidence: XpDump mean bank from r400 to r1600: andli28 3,642 / 2,679 / 2,800 / 3,231; Strequals 2,925 / 2,039 / 2,912 / 3,866; kuma 794-1,762; chenyx512 154-1,538. Ours 195-290 in every tier and phase. Upper-tier spend on digs jumps by 1,800 crumbs in r1800-2000 (the T17 level dump).

Confound: Part of their bank is saved for the end-game level dump (andli28), which is a closed line for us.

## Levers

- **C.STUN_MIN13: spend combat-stun crumbs on big groups (keep the bank for stuns that catch more)**: In Duck.placeCombatTrap (src/bot/Duck.java, the g_iter1 placement branch after the line 'enemies.length < C.STUN_ENEMIES_MIN || rc.getCrumbs() < 100 + C.TRAP_RESERVE'), take tile t toward the enemy centroid as now. Count Duck.enemies within dist2 13 of t. If the count is below C.STUN_MIN13 and rc.getCrumbs() < C.STUN_RICH, skip the build and count it in a new indicator counter 'sg'. Unchanged: carrierStun, defend() rings, damTrap, spendFloat, placement. STUN_MIN13 0 is byte-identical to g_iter7. Arm: STUN_MIN13 5 with STUN_RICH 700; 700 sits below FLOAT_CRUMBS 1500, so a saved bank never feeds float stuns. Dose ladder: 3 (drops only the 0-2 class: 21% of field stuns, 3.36 victims, median latency 36) and 4. Optional stack: with crumbs >= STUN_RICH and 5+ enemies near the tile, build a second stun on the next-best adjacent tile in the same turn (a build-0 stun costs 5 cooldown, so two fit). The mechanism differs from the closed lines. g4front moved placement and lowered the reserve; ct1/ct2 lowered the threshold; the ring reserve and F2 are about flags. This lever only changes which moments get the scarce crumbs. Signature: Census vs g_iter7 twins: stunVictims >= 1.10x; stunVictimsFast/stunVictims 0.43 -> >= 0.50; trapsBuilt 0.85-1.05x (same crumbs); kills >= 0.97x and enemyCaptured <= 1.1x as guards. --trapgeo (field stuns, enemy within dist2 20): share built with 5+ enemies within dist2 13 goes 0.40 -> >= 0.60; victims per built field stun up by >= 0.5 (twins ~4.5); median latency down. XpDump bank mean r400-1500 goes ~230 -> 300-600, capped by STUN_RICH. Pre-registered 5(a), g7sg vs g_iter7 twins on 12 chosen cells: {andli28, kuma, hsmalladi, NotLLeon} x {DefaultMedium, Fountain, Randy}, side A, one pre-written seed (diag-batch). It FIRES iff all of: (a) 'sg' > 0 in 12/12 games; (b) pooled trapgeo share at 5+ enemies >= 0.55, and above the twin in >= 9/12 cells; (c) pooled stunVictims arm/twin >= 1.05, higher in >= 7/12 cells; (d) sum of enemyCaptured <= twins + 2, sum of captured >= twins - 2, overruns 0, exceptions 0. Logged without a bar: kills, deaths, killShare, bank. Delivery: rel:stunVictims>=1.08 nw:enemyCaptured<=1.1 mean:overruns<=0 (no kills guard, PROMPTS 183). Expected: The yield gradient holds against the top four (3.36 / 4.31 / 6.93) and against the rest (2.83 / 3.78 / 6.16), so the effect should be band-wide. Our stuns are crumb-bound. Upper bound is +36% victims per crumb at MIN13 5 (+11% at 3); after waiting costs and leakage into ring rebuilds, expect +10-20% stun victims, about +90-180 a game. At the measured lift of a stun victim's 4-round death rate (+~8 points), that is +7-14 kills a game. A capture effect is modest, likely +0.05 to +0.10 per game; it touches both the upper-tier and rest-of-band fights. Probability of delivering: about 0.4. Main risk: small skirmishes and small raids lose their early stun.
- **C.BUILD_DEFER: let build specialists emerge (defer to a nearby higher-build ally, who multi-builds at its discount). Same economic mechanism as the closed builders family, reached a different way.**: In Duck.placeCombatTrap, before building: if any ally in Duck.allies within dist2 C.BD_R2 (8), not carrying a flag, has buildLevel >= rc's build level + C.BD_GAP (1), and our crumbs are below 2 x (100 + TRAP_RESERVE), skip and count 'bd'. The crumbs stay for the cheaper builder. Multi-build: a robot at build >= 3 builds up to C.BD_MULTI (3) stuns per turn while action cooldown < 10 and crumbs >= its discounted cost + TRAP_RESERVE, on the next-best adjacent tiles toward the centroid. At build 6 a stun costs 3 cooldown. Unchanged: carrierStun, rings, dam traps, attack/heal, setup. There are no setup digs, no designated idx and no attack/heal stop. Those were the three causes of the closed g4builder / g4builder2 / g4econ2 failures (bank drain, non-builders silenced near defenders, -17% kills). Build XP concentrates on whichever robots build most, since the jail penalty takes attack first on ties and so spares build levels up to 3. The upper tier reaches the same price by 64-90 setup digs: that is the closed path, and this lever does not copy it. BUILD_DEFER false is byte-identical to g_iter7. Signature: XpDump per-robot XP (RB rows): robots outside the top three with >= 15 build XP at r2000 go 0.2-0.7 -> >= 3; robots at build >= 3 beyond the defenders by r1000 go ~0 -> >= 2. XpDump TY post-setup stun price 88-91 -> <= 78. Census: trapsBuilt >= 1.10x with spend about equal; stunVictims >= 1.08x; kills >= 0.97x and enemyCaptured <= 1.1x as guards; counter 'bd'. Pre-registered 5(a), g7bd vs g_iter7 twins on the same 12 cells as STUN_MIN13. It FIRES iff all of: (a) 'bd' > 0 in 12/12 games; (b) pooled post-setup stun price arm/twin <= 0.90; (c) >= 2 non-defender robots at build >= 3 by r1000 in >= 8/12 arm games (--levels or the XP reader); (d) pooled stunVictims arm/twin >= 1.05; (e) the same capture, overrun and exception guards as lever 1. Delivery: rel:stunVictims>=1.08 nw:enemyCaptured<=1.1 mean:overruns<=0. Expected: If the top local builders settle at build 3-5, the field-stun price falls about 20-30%. That gives +25-40% field stuns at the same crumbs: about +30-45 stuns and +130-200 victims a game against the upper tier (our 110 field stuns a game there), and the same against the rest. Price is about half of the log stun-count gap to the four strongest bots, so this is the largest economic lever we control. Its causal weight is unproven: the evidence is cross-sectional, Cyril is a counterexample and g4econ2 was band-neutral. Organic builders also die, losing 3-6 build XP per death once build is their top skill. Probability of delivering: about 0.3. It stacks with STUN_MIN13, since multi-build spends the bank that lever saves. Because it reopens the builders family by a different implementation, the owner should confirm the reopen before it is built.
