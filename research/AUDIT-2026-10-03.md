# Audit 2026-10-03: basics behind the Cyril plateau

## 1. Method

This audit was read-only. No project file was changed, nothing was committed, no games were run and the VM queue was not touched. Scope: src/g_iter3, src/bot at its defaults, the live arms with their switches on (g3camp2, g3escrg2, g2cstun, g3def2, g2crb), tools/ and test/.

Sources:
- the code;
- engine/engine.jar, read with javap and decompiled;
- RULES.md;
- existing replays through `tools/replay-dump.sh`, local and on the VM, with at most 3 dumps at once;
- census and survey CSVs (research/fill, gauntlet/*), the 43 delivery PASS/FAIL files, results.csv files, progress/games.csv and survey.csv.

Offline harnesses ran in the session scratchpad: PickupLag, Captured, RelocScan, RelocSim, SetupFlags, beliefs.py/predict.py, HintGeo, Haus, CS, CR, BcTop, elofit, diverge.py, basebias.py and seedmatch.py.

A second, independent pass reproduced the mechanism of every one of the 56 findings: 0 were rejected and 0 left unverified. Population figures that the second pass did not re-run are marked "(not re-run)" and listed in §7. After deduplication the report has 20 bot defects (BOT) and 17 tool or measurement defects (MEAS). The previous audit's IDs are written "old A3", "old B7" and so on.

### BOT1 (major): captured own flags are never recognised (old A13, now active)
Sources: dead-3, rules-4, comms-1, behaviour-5.

**Where:**
- src/g_iter3/Duck.java:497-498: defendTarget() returns `Comms.flagHome(homeFlag())` unconditionally.
- :66: the defender gate.
- :163-166: OF_ALERT and OF_THREAT are raised for all 3 indices.
- :214-221: alertedFlag().
- :193: trySpawn wants the alerted home.
- :382: threat responders.
- Comms.java:5-16: the schema has no captured state for our flags.
- The same code is in src/bot (Duck.java:517-519, 67, 164-167, 215-222, 194, 383) and in every arm. g3def2 has two defenders per flag.

**Broken:** on capture the engine removes the flag (RobotControllerImpl: `flag.setLoc(null); gameWorld.getAllFlags().remove(flag)`). The bot never records this. The flag's defender keeps guarding and trapping the empty home, and alerts on that index keep firing and can win the responder and respawn choice.

**Evidence:**
- Captured.java over gauntlet/20261003-130228-scrim-g_iter3-fill1791032543 (36 replays, re-run): 66 of our flags were captured in 29 games, leaving 41,467 rounds from capture to game end. The defenders built 619 traps after their flag's capture, 8.6% of the team's 7,170 post-setup builds.
- Alligator (`--flags`, `--robot 14055`): A1067 (home (35,29)) captured at r301. Its defender stays at (34-36,28-30) with note 'defend' from r365 to r1330, and its build XP rises from 26 to 35 over r730-r1030.
- TwistedTreeline fill1791033415 botA: A1398 captured at r384. #11253 stays within dist2 8 of (18,24) in 569 of 733 alive rounds, to r1131; build XP rises 1 → 36.
- DefaultMedium botA (run 131700), `--comm 400-2000`: A138 (flag 0) captured at r399. Alerts were then written on flag 0 in 394 rounds, and alertedFlag() would pick flag 0 in 507-514 rounds.
- beliefs.py over 29 replays (partly re-run):
  - in 76% of dead-flag rounds (11,745 of 15,464) the defender stood within dist2 8 of the empty home;
  - 2,874 alert writes went to captured indices;
  - a captured flag was the chosen alert in 2,577 rounds, 11.9% of post-setup rounds in those games.
- Census (1,984 g_iter3 games): at least one Cyril capture in 1,623 (81.8%). Play continues after 1-2 captures in 944 (47.6%).

**Impact:**
- Each capture removes one duck for the rest of the game (two in g3def2): about 630 duck-rounds per capture, about 1,150 per game in the sample.
- About 17 trap builds a game go around nothing, while the bank (110-245 crumbs at r200) is what limits carrier and combat stuns.
- In about 12% of rounds of these games, responders within dist2 100 and every non-defender respawn are steered toward a flag that no longer exists. This is partly mitigated: an empty home still lies next to our spawn.
- These are the games already being lost: with any Cyril capture we win 18%.

**Fix (C.FLAG_LOST):**
1. Keep a shared captured bit per own flag, in a spare slot 58-63 or as an OF_HOME sentinel.
2. Set it when either:
   - (a) the home is in view and empty, with no fresh OF_CARRY and no drop of flag i within the return window (4 rounds, 25 if they hold CAPTURING); or
   - (b) a fresh carrier of flag i was last seen within dist2 13 of an enemy spawn centre and is never sighted again.
3. Once set:
   - skip index i in the alert loop, alertedFlag(), trySpawn and the threat response, and clear OF_THREAT[i];
   - re-home the defender(s) to the nearest live flag, or release them to the field;
   - stop ring traps there, and clear DEST_CAMP camps for i.

**Test:** BotTest with flag 0 marked captured:
1. defendTarget() for idx 0 returns a live home.
2. sense() with an enemy within dist2 20 of home 0 leaves OF_ALERT+0 unchanged.
3. With OF_ALERT = {r, r-1, 0}, alertedFlag() == 1.
4. trySpawn does not choose zone 0 through the alert, and defend() builds nothing near home 0.

Replay contract (new `--capabilities` columns): alertsAfterCapture = defenderRoundsAtCapturedHome = buildsAtCapturedHome = 0.

### BOT3 (major): relocation abandons about 20% of flags before r200
Sources: rules-2, rules-3, behaviour-1.

**Where:**
- src/g_iter3/Duck.java:545-597, relocTargetV2/spotFree: the grid scoring at :557-568 has no passability or reachability test (src/bot :565-593).
- :614-623: relocStall++ runs on every turn the distance did not fall, including turns where Nav.moveTo returns early at Nav.java:37 because movement is not ready.
- C.java:26: RELOC_STALL = 12, commented "before dropping where we stand", although V2 walks home at Duck.java:620.
- test/bot/AuditTest.java relocOk checks only distance and spacing.

**Broken:**
- (a) Spots are chosen by distance from the enemy alone, so they can be walls, water or tiles behind obstacles a carrier cannot cross. A flag holder cannot fill or dig ("Can't fill while holding a flag"), and dams are impassable until r200.
- (b) A carrier gets move cooldown 20 (FLAG_MOVEMENT_COOLDOWN), so it moves every second turn. The 12-"turn" tolerance is therefore 6 moves of detour, after which the flag walks back to its spawn centre and all progress is lost.

**Evidence:**
- RelocScan (the bot's own relocTargetV2, 78 maps × 2 sides, symmetry decided, recompiled against the current src/bot):
  - 468 spots, 442 of them off the centre: 30 on a wall, 52 in water, 3 on a dam;
  - 104 (23.5%) cannot be reached to within dist2 2 over setup-passable land, on 37 maps (Starfish 6/6, Waterworld 6/6, BedWars 6/6, Fusbol 6/6, EvilGrin 4, Divergent 4, Yinyang);
  - the best reachable alternative would add 5.4 tiles on average.
- RelocSim (real g_iter3 classes, each map's terrain, carrier cooldown 20, no other robots; re-run):
  - 119 of 468 flags are abandoned and sent home: 86 with unreachable spots, 33 with reachable ones;
  - counting the stall only on movement-ready turns: 61 abandons (14 reachable);
  - an abandoned flag gives up a median 9.5 tiles (mean 9.3) of distance to the nearest enemy spawn.
- All 1,622 g_iter3-vs-Cyril replays (L6): 957 of 4,866 flags (19.7%) were carried more than 2 tiles and then dropped back within 1 tile of their centre, in 681 games (42%).
- 35-replay join with the scan:
  - 23 of 103 off-centre spots were unreachable, and 15 of those flags ended at most 1 tile from their centre;
  - 11 of 72 reachable spots 3+ tiles away also ended at most 1 tile from the centre (8.2 tiles given up).
- Traces:
  - Hurricane botA `--robot 10657`: mcd alternates 10/20; it reaches (52,21) at r26 (dist2 18 to spot (55,18)), bugs away round a diagonal wall, and the flag is placed at (55,32) at r71.
  - Diagonal botA (151009) `--robot 12846`: reaches (3,25) by r29, oscillates to r62, and is back at (15,36) carrying until r200.
  - Starfish botB: B972 (27,27) → (26,27) and B415 (30,11) → (29,11), whose spots (33,33) and (33,1) are in water.

**Impact:**
- About 1 flag in 5 stays at its centre instead of about 9 tiles farther from the enemy, in 42% of games against the ladder target. CRACK-CYRIL's gradient (captured 82% under 20 tiles, 72% at 20-28, 58% at 28-36) puts roughly 10 points of capture probability on each such flag.
- Decision impact: TRAINING_LOG.md:1219-1227 parked relocation as "a modest lever" on a dose capped by this defect.

**Fix:** two sub-switches, tested separately and together.
- C.RELOC_REACH: score only passable tiles reachable over setup-passable land (BFS over sensed tiles plus terrain memory and symmetric images, spread over turns). Fall back to the best reachable tile; never choose wall, water or dam.
- C.RELOC_STALL_MOVES: count only movement-ready turns (or moves since the best distance), sized to half speed. On give-up, drop at the legal tile reached that is farthest from the enemy centres, not at the centre (legality as in BOT6).
- Fix the C.java:26 comment.

**Test:**
- Map test (RelocScan/RelocSim as a unit test over engine/maps with GameMapIO.loadMap): every spot is passable and reachable within (RELOC_DEADLINE-41)/2 moves, and a simulated carrier places every flag within dist2 2 of its spot by r170. This fails today with 104 and 119 cases.
- Fake controller: a carrier ready every other turn, with a target behind a wall needing 8 non-improving moves; relocStall must not trip before 8 moves.
- Census column relocNoop ≈ 0.

### BOT5 (major): flag pickup only at turn start; fight() strikes before reaching a loose flag
Sources: rules-1, rules-5, dead-8.

**Where:**
- src/g_iter3/Duck.java:32: pickupFlags runs once, before any move.
- :67: heal before moving.
- :80-81: Nav.moveTo then heal, with no pickup.
- :388: walk to a visible enemy flag.
- Micro.java:159-160: strike first if anything is in reach.
- Micro.java:217-222: a post-move pickup exists only under C.REGRAB, and only if the action is still ready.
- src/g3escrg2/C.java:43: REGRAB's stated intent is "pick it up before striking"; the arm's Micro.java:159 and :221 do the opposite.

**Broken:**
- Pickup is an action (assertCanPickupFlag checks range dist2 2, action cooldown < 10, team and dropped round), and InternalRobot keeps action and movement cooldowns apart, so a pickup is legal after a move. The bot never tries one after moving.
- A strike adds action cooldown 20 (RULES.md:41), and acting needs cooldown < 10 (RULES.md:36). A strike therefore blocks a pickup this turn and the next.

**Evidence:**
- PickupLag over run 130228 (36 replays, re-run):
  - 383 post-setup pickups by us;
  - 146 flag-rounds where one of our ducks ended its turn within dist2 2 of a pickable enemy flag with its action free and nobody picked it up (necessarily a duck that arrived during its turn);
  - 258 duck-rounds arrived adjacent and spent the action on an attack or heal;
  - 3 regrab windows were lost with a free adjacent duck, and 30 expired after one of ours had been adjacent.
- Hurricane botA, r402: #13661 at (39,44), acd 0, flag B2740 at (40,45); it picks the flag up only at r403.
- TreeSearch botA: B676 dropped at (27,15) at r423. #10584 ends r427 at (28,14) with acd 4 and note 'figh'; the flag returns home at the end of r427.
- g3escrg2 run 130958 (37 replays): 68 step-adjacent-then-attack cases.
  - Hurricane: B2740 dropped at (45,39) at r233. #11237 attacks at r234 and steps adjacent (acd 20), is not ready at r235, and picks up at r236; the window ended at r237.
- The same metric reads 69 in g_iter3, so strike-first is general and not specific to REGRAB.

**Impact:**
- About 4 avoidable pickup-delay rounds a game, against about 10.6 pickups a game. At Cyril's flags our carriers die a median 5 rounds after the grab, so every extra round spent adjacent is exposure.
- 3 regrabs were lost outright in 36 games.
- In the convoy arm, a strike-first uses 2 of the 4 rounds in which a dropped flag can be regrabbed.

**Fix (C.PICKUP_AFTER_MOVE):**
- When a duck has moved, its action is ready and it is not carrying, call pickupFlags() before any tryAttack or tryHeal: in Micro.fight after the step, and in Duck.turn after Nav.moveTo(ft) and after the crumb and hold moves.
- When a pickable enemy flag is one legal step away, skip the pre-move strike and the pre-move heal at :67: move, pick up, then strike or heal if the action is still free.
- Fix the REGRAB comment.

**Test:** BotTest with a fake controller that models dist2 ≤ 2 pickup and separate cooldowns:
1. Duck at (10,10), enemy flag on the ground at (12,10), injured ally adjacent, no enemies: after Duck.turn(), pickupFlag was called and heal was not.
2. Enemy at dist2 4, loose flag one step away, action ready: after fight(), rc.hasFlag() is true and no attack was made.

Census freeAdjacentNoPickup = 0 and step-adjacent-then-attack = 0 on a diag block.

### BOT7 (minor): carrier stuns are built behind the carrier
Source: behaviour-4.

**Where:** src/g_iter3/Duck.java:720-741 carrierStun. The comment says "Built ahead of the carrier on its way home", but the candidate tiles are G.me.add(d), i.e. within dist2 2 of the builder. Engine assertCanBuild: build range dist2 2, and no build adjacent to an enemy.

**Evidence:**
- CS.java (same-round positions, 36 Cyril replays): 669 qualifying builds (18.6 a game). Four rounds later, 378 of the carriers had stopped carrying. Of the 290 still carrying:
  - 32 (11%) were closer to the trap, 216 (74%) farther and 42 (14%) at the same distance;
  - displacement was toward the trap in 39 cases and away in 191.
- L7, 400 games (not re-run): 32% of these traps caught a carrier when triggered, and 59% triggered with no carrier within dist2 13.

## 6. Systematic tests for this class of problem

1. **An engine-contract fake controller.** One fake RobotController whose rules are pinned against engine bytecode: separate action and move cooldowns, pickup legal after a move, droppedRounds, fill-then-move, build range and enemy adjacency, carrier cooldown 20/12, the 64-char indicator cap, capture removing the flag, and the two placement checks. Every BOT regression test runs on it. Catches BOT3, BOT5, BOT6, BOT8, BOT17 and MEAS5.
2. **A map sweep with the real bot classes** (RelocSim generalised), over all 78 maps × 2 sides. Every destination the bot picks (relocation spots, fallback tiles, explore targets) is passable and reachable; every flag is placed by r170; maximum setup bytecode per turn is reported with the Sym fill separated. Catches BOT3, BOT16 and old A14.
3. **Replay contracts that fail a block.** Shared slots obey their schema: OF_CARRY at r201 = 0 absent an enemy carry; OF_HOME at r205 = the r200 PLACE; no alert on a captured index; no defender rounds or builds at a captured home; 0 r200 resets. No indicator string reaches 64 chars, and no counter rises while the robot is off the map. Catches BOT1, BOT6, BOT14, MEAS5 and MEAS9.
4. **A firing census for every live mechanism.** Each switch in tools/arm-intent.txt names a counter that must appear in replays. A switch that is on with a counter at 0 in every game fails delivery, and a mechanism firing in fewer than 20% of base games cannot be ablated or tuned. Delivery bars are read after the mechanism's spend, not before it. Catches BOT11, BOT12 and MEAS5.
5. **Static checks in unit-tests.sh:**
   - deadcode.py with constant folding and a write-only-field check;
   - the slot layout derived from the Comms constants;
   - a comment-claim lint: every "ahead / before / same turn / N rounds" claim on a constant or mechanism maps to an arm-intent assertion or a harness measurement.

   Catches MEAS14, MEAS15, BOT7 and BOT17.
6. **A per-phase, per-side utility census against the opponent.** Crumbs left and collected per half (r200, r201-400), duck-rounds guarding nothing, defender distance at enemy grabs, and response coverage of every fresh alert. Catches BOT1, BOT2, BOT4 and BOT9.
7. **Statistical tool tests on synthetic data with known effects.** The three-way delivery verdict and power printout, identical share and discordance with every verdict, within-cell base-vs-population z, paired basics, and a non-convergence warning. Plus an identity run after every change that should not alter behaviour. Catches MEAS1, MEAS2, MEAS4, MEAS6 and MEAS11.
8. **Instrument self-tests on fixtures with known truth.** Caught exceptions 'x3', blank vs 0, a repeated cell, an unreadable replay (K of M), and results keyed by code hash. Catches MEAS3, MEAS7, MEAS8 and MEAS10.
