## The owner's symmetry requirement

The requirement holds. On every engine map, symmetry can be settled without guessing after a small, bounded number of observations, so code that cannot do this has a bug. No build meets it today:

- **g_iter1** narrows the candidates using only the broadcast hints from rounds 1-3, then picks ROT > FX > FY in a fixed order. It is wrong on 32.4% of (map, side) pairs (A3). Each map-side is always right or always wrong, because the broadcast draw is seeded by the map.
- **g1sym** (the repair arm) observes walls, spawn zones and setup dams, and counts equivalent candidates as decided. It still has three gaps:
  - While undecided, it still returns the fixed-order guess to every consumer (src/g1sym/Sym.java:72-77).
  - It ignores exact evidence the team already holds: enemy flag ids (A2; `grep -n getID src/g1sym/*.java` shows no decode).
  - A jailed robot's eliminations wait 25+ rounds before they are published (A10).

  Its verification run decided Soccer only at r1059 (TRAINING_LOG.md:956-958).
- **The instrument and the gates** cannot tell deciding from guessing. symOk scores a guess as correct, and both the g1sym delivery gate (TRAINING_LOG.md:961, PASS at exactly 0.90, :975) and tools/basics.py:64 accept 10% wrong (B1).

**What is achievable**, measured over all 78 engine maps and both sides:
- **Before r201:** using immutable evidence only (walls, spawn zones, and dams before r201) plus team memory, 268 of 298 wrong-symmetry cases are eliminated.
- **After setup:** the other 30 cases, on 15 maps, need one post-setup sighting.
- **One enemy flag id** leaves only the true enemy spawn set in 418 of 468 cases and never removes the truth.
- **Equivalent candidates:** on 5 maps the remaining candidates predict the same enemy spawns, so those maps count as decided.

**The bar should be:**
- Never eliminate the truth (symWrong = 0 in every game).
- Decide setup-decidable maps by r201.
- Decide the rest within one or two enemy flag or spawn-zone sightings.
- While undecided, act on the union of candidates, never on a single guess.

**Effect on play:** in g_iter1 the symmetry steers only carrier interception and a last-resort target, and g1sym vs g_iter1 head-to-head was 63-57 (TRAINING_LOG.md:982-984). The fix matters for correctness, and for every later feature that uses enemy destinations, more than for g_iter1's win rate.

## A. Bot behaviour defects

Each fix ships as a switch in src/bot, with the default off so the build plays like g_iter1. It must first pass its delivery mini-block (`tools/delivery-gate.sh`, rule 13), with a diagnostic game showing the mechanism firing (rule 5). Only then is it tested as an arm against g_iter1 before adoption.

### A1. Own-flag alert: wrong meaning, almost always on, switches off every defender and parks the field army (Critical)

**Where.**
- Alert raised: src/g_iter1/Duck.java:52-54 and :57-60.
- Freshness test: :92-99 (alertedFlag, 10 rounds).
- defend() runs only when no flag at all is alerted: :34.
- Non-defenders within dist2 100 of an alerted centre are sent onto the centre tile: :170.
- Respawns go to the alerted zone: :77-79.
- Documented meaning: src/g_iter1/Comms.java:12.
- Same code in src/bot/Duck.java:61, :146-154, :176.

Merges four findings ("Defenders stop running defend()", "Any flag's alert freezes all three defenders", "Our-flag alert fires on any enemy", "Own-flag alert fires on enemies ~9 tiles away").

**Broken.** Comms.java:12 defines slot 10+i as the "round of the latest enemy sighting within 20 of" flag i. The code does something broader:
- :57-60 raise the alert when the observing duck is within dist2 20 of centre i and sees any enemy at all, so the enemy can be up to about dist2 80 from the flag.
- :52-54 alert every centre within dist2 36 of any of our flags that a duck sees while any enemy is in its vision.

Three consumers then over-react:
- All three defenders drop defend() when any one flag is alerted.
- Field ducks walk onto the centre tile and stand there.
- Every respawn goes to the alerted zone.

**Evidence.**
- **Wrong meaning.** Share of writes with an enemy actually within dist2 20 of the flag (scratch probe on a modified ReplayDump; not fully re-derived by the verifier):

  | Replay (team) | Writes with an enemy within dist2 20 |
  |---|---|
  | b2rg-DefaultMedium (B) | 297/1694 |
  | b2rg-Battlecode24 (B) | 513/2217 |
  | matches/p1 (B) | 342/2068 |
  | b2rg-Tunnels (B) | 113/2121 |
  | b3own (B) | 182/3258 |

  A reproduced example: on b2rg-DefaultMedium, `--map-at 450` shows no B robot within dist2 20 of A's flag at (9,27), yet A's slot 12 = 450.
- **Almost always on.** `--comm 1-2000 --team B` with awk over slots 10-12 shows an alert fresh (≤10 rounds old) in this many of 1800 post-setup rounds:

  | Map | Rounds with a fresh alert |
  |---|---|
  | b2rg-Battlecode24 | 1582 |
  | DefaultMedium | 1057 |
  | DefaultLarge | 1188 |
  | b3own | 1686 |

  On b2rg-Tunnels, slot 10 equals the current round in all 1206 rounds from r195 to r1400.
- **Defenders off.**
  - On b2rg-Battlecode24 (`--logs '.' --team B`), defenders B#13994, #11633 and #11727 have note 'defend' in 220/220/221 of 1800 post-setup rounds. That is exactly 1800 minus the rounds with an alert on any flag.
  - On b3site1, #11633 stands on its own centre (17,42) from r282 to r1328 (1047 rounds) with mcd 0 and an empty note. Its own flag's slot s11 stays 0 throughout.
- **Ring never rebuilt.** On b2rg-Battlecode24, B's ring explosives were triggered at (18,42) r826, (33,51) r833, (45,37) r948, (16,41) r1000 and (18,41) r1015. Only (18,42) was rebuilt (r1154). From r1300 the bank was above 700, but flag 1's alert was fresh in 700/700 rounds and two defenders had an empty note in 700/700 rounds.
- **Parking.**
  - b2rg-Tunnels `--robot 10496` (idx 46, not a defender) stands on (19,4), our flag's home, from r200 to r1363 with hp 1000 and an empty note.
  - b3site1 #10880 stands on flag 0's tile (45,38) from r608 to r1159.
  - The longest parks over 18 local g_iter1 replays are 1238 rounds (b3own #13086), 773 (b2rg-Battlecode24) and 630 (p1).
  - Standing on the flag tile protects nothing. assertCanPickupFlag (RobotControllerImpl.java:851) checks range, team and drop round, not whether the tile is occupied.
- **Offence stalled on Tunnels.** g_iter1 first sees an enemy flag at r1674 (b2rg) and r1299 (b2rgu), against r269-627 on every other local map. It makes 2 pickups all game (r1710, r1797). research/census-ctl-p0.csv records firstFlagSight 1748 and 1197 on Tunnels.

**Impact.**
- The flag-defence routine (ring traps and ring walk) is off for 42-100% of defender time.
- Respawns pile onto one zone (Tunnels: 118/118).
- A quarter to four fifths of non-defender robot-rounds sit inside the pull radius (scratch BehAudit, not independently re-derived).
- On maps where a spawn lies within about 9 tiles of the enemy line, the alert never lapses and the offence does not start for 1100+ rounds.

The verifiers graded this major on open maps (on DefaultMedium the pull is large at r500 and small at r1000-1500) and critical on Tunnels-like maps. It ranks first because it is the largest measured loss of robot time.

**Fix.**
1. Raise slot 10+i only when some enemy's location is within dist2 20 of flag i's home: loop over the enemies, not over the observer's position. In the flag-seen path, map the flag to its own index with `ourFlagIndex(f.getID())`.
2. Gate each defender on its own flag's alert, not on `alertedFlag() < 0`.
3. Store the sighting location next to the round, and send responders to that location, stopping at contact. Never use the flag tile itself as a destination.
4. Route respawns only on the tightened alert.

**Regression test.**
- Unit tests with the fake RobotController:
  - Observer at dist2 4 from home, enemy at dist2 50 from home: OF_ALERT is unchanged.
  - Enemy at dist2 16 from home: OF_ALERT == round.
  - OF_ALERT[0] = round, robot is defender idx 1, no enemy in view: the turn takes the defend() path (note 'defend').
- Replay census columns, with bars:
  - alertWithoutThreat = 0.
  - defendShare ≥ 95% (defender turns in defend() when its own alert is stale).
  - maxParkOnCentre < 50 rounds.
  - firstFlagSight < 600 on a Tunnels diagnostic.

### A2. Enemy flag ids give the exact enemy spawn centre, but no build decodes them (Major)

**Where.**
- src/g_iter1/Comms.java:30-48 (enemyFlagSlot/reportEnemyFlag store id+1 in slots 1-3) and :60-64 (syncSym).
- src/bot/Comms.java:47-65, :77-81.
- src/g1sym/Comms.java:42, :60-64.

**Broken.** The engine creates each flag as `new Flag(team, indexToLocation(i), i)` (reference engine GameWorld.java:123; javap GameWorld offsets 502-509), where locationToIndex = x + y*W. A flag's id is therefore the location index of its spawn centre. Comms.ourFlagIndex already relies on this for our own flags. Every enemy flag sighting names an enemy spawn centre exactly, and the id is stored in slots 1-3. No build turns it back into a location: grep for getID over src/g_iter1, src/bot and src/g1sym finds only slot allocation, ourFlagIndex and pickup.

**Evidence.**
- b2rg-Battlecode24, `--comm 1-2000 --team A`:
  - s1 = 2496 at r367, i.e. id 2495 = (17,42). Later ids decode to (45,38) and (32,52).
  - All three are FY images of A's centres, yet s16 = 5 (ROT|FY) for all 2000 rounds. So best() = ROT, and A's enemy centres are wrong for all 1634 rounds after the first id appeared.
- b3own, team A: id 3100 at r345 = (32,52), which only FY fits. A's centres are wrong for 1656 rounds.
- In 14 local team-games, the first id alone left exactly the true symmetry, or its equivalence class.
- Over engine/maps (IdSym):
  - One id leaves only the true enemy spawn set in 418/468 (map, side, flag) cases.
  - The other 50 (Anchor, Bunkers, Soccer, Yinyang, ...) leave two candidates.
  - The truth is never removed.

**Impact.** The exact answer to the owner's question is already in the shared array and is ignored. In g_iter1 a wrong symmetry steers only carrier interception (Duck.java:183-189) and the last-resort target (:216), so this is major rather than critical. The cost grows with every feature that uses enemy destinations (HOLD_LINE, relocTarget, Track, the crack's CUT).

**Fix.** Run the same elimination in three places: when enemyFlagSlot allocates a new slot, in syncSym for every nonzero EF_ID, and at the sighting itself in sense().
1. Decode c = (id % W, id / W).
2. Remove every symmetry under which no own spawn centre maps exactly onto c.
3. AND the result into Sym.cands and slot 16. Never leave the set empty; count a contradiction instead.

**Regression test.**
- BotTest: a 59x59 fake map with our centres at (32,6), (17,16) and (45,20), and cands = 7. `reportEnemyFlag(FlagInfo((17,42), them, false, 17+42*59))` must leave Sym.cands == FY and slot 16 == 4.
- Replay check: after a decoded EF_ID first appears, the number of rounds in which slot 16's best() fails to map an own centre onto it must be 0. It is now 1634 on b2rg-Battlecode24 A and 1656 on b3own A.

### A3. Symmetry is guessed, not determined (Major)

**Where.**
- src/g_iter1/Sym.java:31-36: fixed ROT > FX > FY order.
- Sym.java:39-53 with Duck.java:21: only the broadcast hints from rounds 1-3 are used.
- Sym.java:56-68: observe() has no caller; its co-visibility test is at :63.
- src/g1sym/Sym.java:72-77 and src/bot/Sym.java:72-77: best() still uses the fixed order while undecided.
- src/bot/Sym.java:25: OBSERVE = false.

**Broken.**
- g_iter1 guesses after the broadcast hints. Its terrain check observe() is never called, and it could not work if it were: it compares walls only, and only when a tile and its image are inside one robot's dist2 20 vision at the same time (:63). That happens only near an axis or the centre.
- g1sym fixes the observing side, but every consumer still gets the fixed-order guess until a decision is reached.

**Evidence.**
- **How the hints are drawn.** At the start of r1, each broadcast is redrawn as a uniform on-map tile within dist2 100 of its flag, using the map-seeded RNG (GameWorld.java:520-544).
- **Error rate of the hint-and-guess method.** An exact computation of fromBroadcasts + best() over 78 maps (SymReach) gives a mean P(wrong enemy spawn set) of 0.324 over 156 map-sides. Examples: Soccer 0.99, Anchor 0.97, HungerGames 0.96, AceOfSpades 0.83, Tunnels 0.72, Battlecode24 0.45.
- **observe() cannot reach some pairs.** It can never eliminate 31 of 151 wrong (map, symmetry) pairs, for example AceOfSpades ROT, Tunnels ROT, Digging ROT, and Snake ROT and FX. On Soccer and Gauntlet the walls are identical under all three symmetries.
- **What immutable evidence decides.**
  - With team memory, 268/298 wrong-symmetry cases are decidable before r201 (SetupDecideDam).
  - The other 30 need a post-setup sighting, on 15 maps: Asteroids, Digging, FloodGates, Fountain, Gauntlet, HungerGames, MIT, MazeRunner, Puzzle, Snake, Soccer, Tunnels, Valentine and Waterworld (ROT is the wrong candidate), and Fusbol (FX).
  - On Battlefield, Canals, DefaultLarge, Hockey and Intercontinental, the surviving candidates give the same enemy spawn set (MapScan).
- **g1sym's own verification.** Verification 6: symOk 8/10 at r250; Snake decided at r253 and Soccer at r1059 (TRAINING_LOG.md:956-958).
- **Not a defect.** Spawn-centre detection (G.init) matches the engine on all 78 maps, so ourFlagIndex is sound.

**Impact.** About a third of g_iter1's games use wrong enemy centres for interception and for the last-resort target. In g1sym, the guess is visible to every consumer for as long as a map stays undecided.

**Fix.**
1. Eliminate on single-tile spawn evidence in both directions:
   - a sensed enemy spawn tile whose image is not one of ours;
   - the image of one of our spawn tiles, seen in vision, that is not an enemy spawn tile.
2. Compare only immutable features, through team memory: walls, spawn zones, and dams only between two sightings made before r201. Never compare water.
3. Treat the problem as decided when every surviving candidate maps our spawns onto the same enemy set. g1sym already does this through collapseEquivalent.
4. While undecided, never return a single symmetry. Consumers take the union of candidate centres, or a broadcast-likelihood weighting that is labelled as a probability.
5. Keep observing after r200, add A2, and publish every turn (A10).

**Regression test.** test/bot/SymTest, run on every engine map (GameMapIO.loadMap) and both sides:
- (a) Feed the full immutable terrain through the fake controller. The truth is never eliminated, and the predicted enemy spawn set equals the engine's.
- (b) Restrict observations to tiles visible from setup-reachable tiles. Every map outside the 15 is decided by r200.
- (c) decided() is true on the 5 equivalent maps.
- (d) While undecided, no consumer receives a single-symmetry answer.

### A4. Any visible enemy takes the whole turn, even behind walls or water (Major)

**Where.** src/g_iter1/Duck.java:28-33: when enemies.length > 0, the turn runs Micro.fight and returns. Micro.java:48-97 has no reachability term. RULES.md: vision is dist2 20 and ignores walls.

**Evidence.**
- b2rg-Tunnels `--robot 11291` stays at (16,29) from r959 to r1122 (164 rounds) with hp 1000, mcd 0 and acd 0, and notes 'fight e1 a2' to 'fight e4 a2'.
- `--map-at 1000` shows the A robots at (11-12,29), behind the wall at x=14-15 that runs from y=19 to y=29. The nearest gap is about 11 rows away.
- Scratch probe: robot-rounds where every visible enemy is unreachable or needs 4+ extra moves, and the robot did nothing (verifier reproduced the example, not these totals):

  | Replay | Idle robot-rounds | Share of enemy-in-vision robot-rounds that were locked |
  |---|---|---|
  | Tunnels | 21,818 | 36.1% |
  | b3own | 19,567 | 26.2% |
  | b2rg-Battlecode24 | 13,087 | 19.6% |
  | DefaultMedium | 9,435 | 16.1% |
  | Open DefaultLarge | — | 0.1% |

**Impact.** Thousands of robot-rounds per game are lost on maps with walls or water lines. Together with A1, this is why Tunnels never develops an attack.

**Fix.** Before Duck.java:28, keep only enemies that can be engaged soon: within dist2 8, or reachable by a short land path (a BFS of radius 4-5 over the sensed MapInfo). If none is left, still heal and build traps, then continue to fieldTarget().

**Regression test.**
- Unit test: the only enemy is at dist2 13 behind a 2-wide wall. fight() is not entered, and Nav advances toward the target.
- Census column lockedIdle, below 2% on the Tunnels, DefaultMedium and Battlecode24 diagnostics.

### A5. EF_STATE=1 ("carried by us") never expires when our carrier dies (Major)

**Where.**
- src/g_iter1/Comms.java:46-47: the only way back to 0 is a ground sighting.
- Duck.java:201: fieldTargetFrom skips any nonzero state.
- src/bot/Comms.java:58-65; Duck.java:103, :275-286, :310-321, :447.

**Evidence.**
- b2rg-Battlecode24 `--flags`:
  - r949: PICKUP B2287 by A#13068 at (45,36).
  - r949: PLACE at (45,36), so the carrier was killed the same round.
  - r953: PLACE at (45,38), back home.
- `--comm --team A`: slot 8 is 1 from r949 to r2000 (1052 rounds), while slots 7 and 9 stay 0. A lost 0-1 on MORE_FLAG_CAPTURES.
- This was observed on a src/bot build; the code in g_iter1 is identical.
- Related case: a pickup made inside our own spawn zone captures at once, so carryFlag never runs and the slot stays 1 instead of 2.

**Impact.** The enemy flag the army already reached drops out of fieldTargetFrom and flankTarget. Because the army is no longer sent there, nobody sees the flag on the ground, so the stale state reinforces itself.

**Fix.**
- At the top of Duck.turn, before the spawn check: if `carriedFlagId >= 0 && !rc.isSpawned()`, set EF_STATE = 0, EF_LOC = the last carried location (A6 then applies the return-home rule), and carriedFlagId = -1.
- In pickupFlags, if `!rc.hasFlag()` right after a pickup, call enemyFlagCaptured.

**Regression test.**
- BotTest: carriedFlagId set to a registered id with EF_STATE = 1, and isSpawned() = false. After Duck.turn, EF_STATE == 0.
- Replay metric st1_not_carried: no episode longer than 2 rounds (now 1052).

### A6. EF_LOC keeps the drop tile after the flag has returned home (Major)

**Where.** src/g_iter1/Duck.java:197-212; src/bot/Comms.java:58-74; src/bot/Duck.java:443-457.

**Broken.** RULES.md says a dropped flag returns to its start after 4 end-of-round ticks (25 if the carrier's team has CAPTURING). The registry does not model this. The drop tile is cleared only in two cases:
- a non-fighting duck within dist2 4 of the tile sees it empty;
- some duck sees the flag at home.

**Evidence.**
- matches/p1 `--flags`: r904 PLACE B1256 at (18,28) (the carrier died), and r908 back home at (41,27). `--comm --team A` shows EF_LOC = 1181, i.e. (18,28), with state 0 from r904 to r1301: 397 rounds. A (c6pair) lost on MORE_FLAG_CAPTURES.
- g_iter1 on b2rgu-Tunnels: r1375 PLACE A635 at (12,18), and r1379 back home at (5,21). B's EF_LOC stays 787, i.e. (12,18), from r1375 to r1497.
- Further episodes: b2rg-Battlecode24 A, 105 rounds; b2rg-DefaultMedium B, 43 rounds.

**Impact.** After each failed carry, the army walks back to where our carrier died, often on our own half or into a standing fight, instead of to the flag.

**Fix.**
- Keep each enemy flag's home in a spare slot (49-63): the decoded id centre (A2), or the first post-setup ground sighting if the enemy relocated the flag.
- When a flag is seen on the ground away from home, store the drop round. Once round > drop + 4 (+21 with our CAPTURING), set EF_LOC to the home.
- In sense(), clear the drop tile when it is seen empty from any distance.

**Regression test.**
- BotTest: report flag X on the ground at P ≠ home H at round r0. At round r0+5, fieldTargetFrom returns H.
- Replay metric st0_wrong_loc: no episode longer than 5 rounds.

### A7. Nav drops its bug state and stall exit whenever the target moves (Major; frequency unmeasured)

**Where.**
- src/g_iter1/Nav.java:31 (`if (target == null || !t.equals(target)) reset(t);`) and :18 (reset()).
- Header comment at :8.
- Callers with a moving target:
  - Duck.java:167-168: the carrier target.
  - Duck.java:174: escort target `r.location`.
  - Duck.java:156: carrier home = G.nearest, with a random tie-break at G.java:57.
- Same code in src/bot/Nav.java:18, :31, :36.

**Broken.** The header promises "no progress for STALL turns flips handedness and resets". But reset() clears bugging and noProgress, and restores id-parity handedness, on any change of target. So for a target that moves, neither bug-following nor the stall flip can ever build up.

**Evidence.** The offline mock-RobotController harness was run against the unmodified g_iter1 classes (byte-identical copies checked with diff). The robot starts in a U-shaped cup at (10,12) with the target north of it:

| Target | Escaped? | Turns | max noProgress | End position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Static at (10,26) | Yes | 31 | 20 | — |
| Alternating (10,26)/(11,26) | No | 200 | 0 | [7,17] / [14,16] |

In games, a carrier moves about every other turn, and the carrier's home tile changes only within the span of the 3x3 spawn block. Even so, the state is reset long before STALL = 20 can fire.

**Impact.** Chasers, escorts and possibly carriers navigate greedily, with no wall-following and no stall exit, so one concave wall stops them. This fits, but does not prove, TRAINING_LOG 2026-10-02: 47% of their captured trips had fewer than 0.5 of our ducks within dist2 20 of the carrier.

**Fix.**
- Keep the bug state when the new target is within dist2 8 of the old one; reset() only on a real change of goal.
- Keep the stall flip until progress resumes.
- Choose the carrier's home once per trip, with a deterministic tie-break.

**Regression test.** In the cup map, a target moving one tile per turn must be escaped within 60 turns, and noProgress must reach STALL at least once. Add this to tools/unit-tests.sh. Add a census of chaser-distance stalls.

### A8. Dam traps and float traps never fire on most maps; their ablations measured no-ops (Major, for decisions)

**Where.**
- damTrap needs crumbs ≥ STUN 100 + C.DAM_TRAP_RESERVE 600 = 700 during r185-200: src/g_iter1/Duck.java:115, :251-253; C.java:15-16.
- The defender ring spends first and keeps only C.DEF_TRAP_RESERVE = 100: C.java:14, Duck.java:239. FILL_RESERVE = 0 (C.java:12).
- spendFloat needs a bank > 1500: Duck.java:38, :268-277. Comment at C.java:18: "self-play floated 13k by r1500".
- Duck.damTraps is written but never read (Duck.java:248, :262).

**Evidence.** `while ! mkdir -p g && mkdir g/$R` parses as `(! mkdir -p) && mkdir`, which is always false. A test with g/RUN1 pre-created ran 0 iterations and reused the existing directory. :78 then truncates results.raw. The fallback would also have dropped TAG.

**Impact.** Two runs with the same TAG started in the same second would merge into one directory, and the first run's results would be lost. The one-at-a-time queue makes this unlikely.

**Fix.** `mkdir -p gauntlet; until mkdir "gauntlet/$RUN_ID" 2>/dev/null; do sleep 1; RUN_ID=...${TAG:+-$TAG}; done`.

**Regression test.** With the stamp directory pre-created, id allocation produces a new directory that still ends in `-<TAG>`.
